# Meth02 Structural Biology – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Meth02 Structural Biology]] · Solutions: [[Meth02 Structural Biology - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Methods]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮.

## Level 1 – Recall
1. Convert: 3 Å to nm; 0.2 nm to Å. Which resolution is needed to see individual side chains?
2. What is the "phase problem" in X-ray crystallography?
3. Name two strengths of NMR compared to X-ray and cryo-EM.
4. What do ESI and MALDI stand for, and what is the difference?
5. What does MS/MS add compared to a single MS measurement?

## Level 2 – Calculation
6. Bragg's law: X-rays with $\lambda = 1.5\,\text{Å}$ hit lattice planes at $\theta = 30°$ (first order, $n = 1$). Compute $d$.
7. For the same λ, which plane spacing gives a reflection at $\theta = 90°$? Why is this the theoretical best resolution for that wavelength?
8. NMR: compute the ¹H Larmor frequency in a 14.1 T magnet (γ/2π ≈ 42.6 MHz/T, round sensibly). And in a 3 T clinical MRI scanner?
9. A protein of 20,000 Da carries 20 extra protons (z = +20) after ESI. Compute $m/z$ (H⁺ ≈ 1 Da).
10. A peptide is phosphorylated on one serine. By how much does its mass increase? What would MS/MS tell you in addition?

## Level 3 – Choose the method
11. Choose the best method: a) structure of a 300 kDa heteromeric GABA_A receptor with a drug bound, b) dynamics of a 15 kDa intrinsically disordered region, c) which proteins change their phosphorylation after fear conditioning, d) a 1.5 Å structure of a small soluble enzyme with its inhibitor, e) structure of tau filaments from a patient's brain.
12. AlphaFold predicts a high-confidence structure for an ion channel. Why might you still need cryo-EM?

## Level 4 – Neuro transfer
13. Explain how the KcsA structure answered the old question of why K⁺ channels are selective for K⁺ over the smaller Na⁺.
14. Cryo-EM showed that tau filaments from Alzheimer's disease and Pick's disease have different folds. Why is this important for diagnosis and therapy?
15. 🧮 A tryptic peptide has a monoisotopic mass of 1,234.6 Da. Compute $m/z$ for charge states 1+, 2+ and 3+ ($m/z = (M + z\cdot1.007)/z$).
