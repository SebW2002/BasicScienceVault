# Meth03 Electrophysiology – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Meth03 Electrophysiology]] · Solutions: [[Meth03 Electrophysiology - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Methods]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮.

## Level 1 – Recall
1. Name the four main patch-clamp configurations and what each measures.
2. What is the difference between voltage clamp and current clamp?
3. What is the main physiological source of the EEG signal? Why do action potentials contribute little?
4. Which frequency range is used for spikes and which for the LFP?

## Level 2 – Understanding
5. Why is a gigaseal necessary for single-channel recordings?
6. Which configuration would you choose to test whether intracellular Ca²⁺ directly opens a channel? Why?
7. In whole-cell mode, the cell is "dialysed" by the pipette solution. Give one advantage and one disadvantage. What is the alternative?
8. Why does MEG see sources in sulci better than sources on gyral crowns? Why does EEG see both?

## Level 3 – Calculation
9. In current clamp, injecting −50 pA hyperpolarises a neuron by 10 mV. Compute the input resistance.
10. With that input resistance and a capacitance of 100 pF, compute $\tau$.
11. A seal of 2 GΩ at a holding potential of −70 mV: what leak current flows through the seal (assume 0 mV reversal)? Compare with a single-channel current of 2 pA.
12. Nyquist: what is the minimum sampling rate for a) a spike with frequency components up to 5 kHz, b) EEG gamma up to 100 Hz?
13. An ERP has an amplitude of 5 µV, and background EEG noise per trial is 20 µV (SD). How many trials are needed to get the noise in the average down to 1 µV?

## Level 4 – Method choice & interpretation
14. Choose a method: a) seizure focus localisation before surgery, b) kinetics of a single NMDA receptor channel, c) activity of 500 neurons across hippocampus and cortex during navigation in a mouse, d) millisecond-scale language processing in healthy participants, e) sleep staging.
15. An extracellular electrode shows two spike waveforms with different amplitudes. Why can't you simply assume they come from two different neuron *types*?
16. 🧮 A Neuropixels probe records 384 channels at 30 kHz with 16-bit samples. How many GB of data per hour?
