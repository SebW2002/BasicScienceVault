# Meth01 Microscopy – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Meth01 Microscopy]] · Solutions: [[Meth01 Microscopy - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Methods]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮.

## Level 1 – Recall
1. State the Abbe resolution limit and define each symbol.
2. What does the pinhole in a confocal microscope do?
3. Why can two-photon microscopy image deeper in brain tissue than confocal microscopy? (Two reasons.)
4. Name three super-resolution methods and their approximate resolution.
5. What is the difference between TEM and SEM?

## Level 2 – Calculation
6. Compute $d$ for $\lambda = 500\,\text{nm}$ and NA = 1.25. Can you resolve two synaptic vesicles 50 nm apart?
7. An oil-immersion objective ($n = 1.5$) has a half-angle of $\theta = 70°$ ($\sin70°\approx0.94$). Compute NA. Can a dry objective (in air) ever have NA > 1?
8. In two-photon microscopy, fluorescence ∝ $I^2$. If you double the laser power, by what factor does the signal increase? Why is this good for sectioning but risky for photodamage?
9. In STED, $d = \frac{d_0}{\sqrt{1 + I/I_\text{sat}}}$. With $d_0 = 200\,\text{nm}$, what $I/I_\text{sat}$ is needed for 20 nm?
10. Localisation precision ≈ $\sigma/\sqrt N$. With $\sigma = 100\,\text{nm}$: precision for $N = 100$ and $N = 10\,000$ photons?

## Level 3 – Choose the method
11. For each question, choose the most suitable method and justify: a) dendritic spine turnover over weeks in an awake mouse, b) whether two proteins are in the same synaptic vesicle, c) the structure of an AMPA receptor bound to a drug, d) all synaptic connections in 1 mm³ of cortex, e) the 3D organisation of vesicles at an active zone in their native state.
12. Expansion microscopy swells tissue 4×. A confocal resolves 250 nm. What is the effective resolution? Name one possible artefact.

## Level 4 – Concepts
13. Why must samples be frozen *very* fast in cryo-EM? What would happen with slow freezing?
14. Cryo-EM single-particle analysis reaches near-atomic resolution although each individual image is extremely noisy. How?
15. What is the "missing wedge" in cryo-ET, and how does it affect the reconstruction?
16. 🧮 Light in cortex attenuates roughly as $I(z) = I_0e^{-z/\ell}$ with scattering length $\ell\approx200\,\mu\text{m}$ at 920 nm. What fraction of ballistic light reaches 600 µm? What does this mean for laser power at depth?
