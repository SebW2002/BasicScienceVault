## Classic misconceptions and interpretation traps (pre-filled)
| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Check / why | Topic |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Higher magnification = higher resolution" | resolution set by λ and NA; magnification beyond that is "empty" | $d = \lambda/2\text{NA}$ | [[Meth01 Microscopy]] |
| "Two-photon uses two lasers" | two photons from **one** pulsed IR laser absorbed simultaneously | – | [[Meth01 Microscopy]] |
| "Colocalisation in confocal = interaction" | only proximity within ~200 nm | super-res, PLA, FRET | [[Meth01 Microscopy]] |
| "Cryo-EM = EM of frozen tissue sections" | vitrified (non-crystalline) ice; SPA averages purified particles; cryo-ET for cells | – | [[Meth01 Microscopy]] |
| "Resolution 2 Å is worse than 4 Å" | smaller number = better | – | [[Meth02 Structural Biology]] |
| "AlphaFold makes experiments unnecessary" | static prediction; states, ligands, complexes need experiments | – | [[Meth02 Structural Biology]] |
| "MS measures mass directly" | measures $m/z$ | charge states | [[Meth02 Structural Biology]] |
| "Whole-cell = cell-attached" | whole-cell ruptures the patch | – | [[Meth03 Electrophysiology]] |
| "Voltage clamp measures voltage" | it **controls** voltage, measures current | – | [[Meth03 Electrophysiology]] |
| "EEG measures action potentials" | mainly synchronous postsynaptic currents of pyramidal cells | – | [[Meth03 Electrophysiology]] |
| "MEG sees all sources" | blind to radial dipoles | cross product | [[Meth03 Electrophysiology]] |
| "Sampling at 2× the frequency of interest is enough" | > 2× the **highest** frequency present (anti-alias filter!) | Nyquist | [[Meth03 Electrophysiology]] |
| "BOLD = neuronal firing" | indirect: oxygenation via neurovascular coupling; closer to synaptic input/LFP | – | [[Meth04 In Vivo Imaging]] |
| "Activation in fMRI means the region is necessary" | correlation only | lesion / TMS | [[Meth04 In Vivo Imaging]] |
| "BOLD increase because more deoxy-Hb" | **less** deoxy-Hb (flow ≫ consumption) | – | [[Meth04 In Vivo Imaging]] |
| "Calcium imaging shows spikes 1:1 with ms timing" | slow, nonlinear proxy; deconvolution estimates spikes | – | [[Meth04 In Vivo Imaging]] |
| "Lower raclopride binding = fewer D2 receptors" | can also mean **more** dopamine release (competition) | – | [[Meth04 In Vivo Imaging]] |
| "Lower Ct = less mRNA" | lower Ct = **more** template | – | [[Meth05 Molecular Biology Tools]] |
| fold change $= 2^{\Delta\Delta\text{Ct}}$ | $2^{-\Delta\Delta\text{Ct}}$ | – | [[Meth05 Molecular Biology Tools]] |
| "A band at the right size proves antibody specificity" | need knockout control | – | [[Meth05 Molecular Biology Tools]] |
| "NpHR is a channel" | it's a Cl⁻ **pump**; ChR2 is a channel | – | [[Meth05 Molecular Biology Tools]] |
| "Optogenetic activation shows a region normally causes behaviour" | shows sufficiency under artificial conditions; test necessity too | – | [[Meth05 Molecular Biology Tools]] |
| "CNO is inert" | back-converts to clozapine | DREADD-free controls | [[Meth05 Molecular Biology Tools]] |
| "Bulk RNA-seq change = gene regulation" | could be cell-composition change | scRNA-seq | [[Meth06 Omics and Sequencing]] |
| "p < 0.05 for 20,000 genes means all are real" | ~1000 false positives expected; use FDR | – | [[Meth06 Omics and Sequencing]] |
| "UMAP distances are meaningful" | UMAP preserves local neighbourhoods, not global distances | – | [[Meth06 Omics and Sequencing]] |
| "A connectome tells you how the circuit works" | gives wiring, not strengths, signs, dynamics or neuromodulation | – | [[Meth06 Omics and Sequencing]] |
