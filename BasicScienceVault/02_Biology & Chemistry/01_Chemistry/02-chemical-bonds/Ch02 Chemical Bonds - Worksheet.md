# Ch02 Chemical Bonds – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Ch02 Chemical Bonds]] · Solutions: [[Ch02 Chemical Bonds - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Chemistry]]
> No calculator needed.

## Level 1 – Recall
1. Order by strength: hydrogen bond, covalent C–C bond, van der Waals interaction.
2. How many hydrogen bonds are there in an A–T and in a G–C base pair?
3. Name the bond type: a) H–H b) Na⁺ Cl⁻ in a crystal c) Zn²⁺–Cys in a zinc finger d) between the two strands of DNA
4. What is the difference between a single, a double and a triple bond with respect to length and rotation?

## Level 2 – Understanding
5. Both CO₂ and H₂O contain polar bonds. Why is only water a polar molecule?
6. Why is the bond between Na⁺ and Cl⁻ very strong in a salt crystal but weak in water?
7. Explain the hydrophobic effect in two sentences. Is it an attraction between nonpolar molecules?
8. Why is the peptide bond planar, and what does this mean for protein structure?
9. Identify the hydrogen-bond donors and acceptors in water and in an N–H···O=C interaction.

## Level 3 – Apply
10. A DNA segment has 40 % GC. A second one of the same length has 60 % GC. Which one needs a higher temperature to separate the strands? Why?
11. $RT\approx2.6\,\text{kJ/mol}$ at 37 °C. Which of these interactions can thermal motion break regularly: a covalent C–C bond (350 kJ/mol), a hydrogen bond (20 kJ/mol), a van der Waals contact (1 kJ/mol)? What does that mean for biology?
12. Coulomb's force scales with $\frac{1}{\varepsilon r^2}$. By what factor does the force between two ions change if a) the distance is doubled, b) the ions move from water ($\varepsilon\approx80$) into a protein interior ($\varepsilon\approx4$)?

## Level 4 – Neuro transfer
13. Why is it advantageous for a neurotransmitter to bind its receptor via many weak interactions rather than one covalent bond?
14. Organophosphate nerve agents bind acetylcholinesterase covalently. Predict the effect on synaptic ACh and on muscles.
15. The real substrate of the Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase is Mg·ATP. What type of bond holds Mg²⁺ to the phosphate groups?
