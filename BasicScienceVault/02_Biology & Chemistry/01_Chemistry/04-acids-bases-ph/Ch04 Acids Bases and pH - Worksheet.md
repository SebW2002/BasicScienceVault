# Ch04 Acids, Bases and pH – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Ch04 Acids Bases and pH]] · Solutions: [[Ch04 Acids Bases and pH - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Chemistry]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮. Useful: $\log_{10}2\approx0.3$, $\log_{10}3\approx0.48$, $\log_{10}5\approx0.7$.

## Level 1 – pH basics
1. What is the pH of a solution with $[\text{H}^+] = 10^{-3}\,\text{M}$? And of $10^{-8}\,\text{M}$?
2. What is $[\text{H}^+]$ at pH 5.5 compared with pH 7.5 (factor)?
3. A solution has pOH 4. What is its pH?
4. Name the conjugate base of H₂CO₃ and the conjugate acid of NH₃.

## Level 2 – Henderson–Hasselbalch
5. Acetic acid (p$K_a$ = 4.76) at pH 4.76: what is the ratio [A⁻]/[HA]?
6. A buffer with p$K_a$ = 7.0 contains 10 times more A⁻ than HA. What is the pH?
7. At pH 7.4, is a group with p$K_a$ = 9.4 mostly protonated or deprotonated? What is the ratio?
8. Which charge does glutamate carry at pH 7.4? (α-COOH p$K_a$ ≈ 2.2, side-chain COOH ≈ 4.3, α-NH₃⁺ ≈ 9.7)
9. Which lab buffer would you choose for an experiment at pH 7.4: acetate (4.76), HEPES (7.5) or Tris (8.1)? Why?

## Level 3 – Bicarbonate
10. Compute blood pH for $[\text{HCO}_3^-] = 24\,\text{mM}$ and $p\text{CO}_2 = 40\,\text{mmHg}$.
11. A patient hyperventilates: pCO₂ falls to 20 mmHg (HCO₃⁻ still 24 mM). New pH? Name the disturbance.
12. What happens to brain blood flow and neuronal excitability in task 11?

## Level 4 – Neuro transfer
13. **pHluorin:** assume p$K_a$ = 7.0 and that only the deprotonated form fluoresces. Inside the vesicle (pH 5) vs. after fusion (pH 7): what fraction fluoresces in each case? By what factor does the signal increase?
14. **Local anaesthetic:** lidocaine is a weak base with p$K_a$ ≈ 7.9. Why does it work less well in inflamed (acidic) tissue?
15. Why can hyperventilation provoke absence seizures during an EEG recording?
16. 🧮 Lidocaine at pH 7.4: compute the fraction in the uncharged form B (base form), using $\frac{[\text{B}]}{[\text{BH}^+]} = 10^{\text{pH}-\text{p}K_a}$.
