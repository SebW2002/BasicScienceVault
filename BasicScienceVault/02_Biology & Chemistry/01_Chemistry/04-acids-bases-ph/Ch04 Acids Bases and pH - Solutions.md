# Ch04 Acids, Bases and pH – Solutions

> Worksheet: [[Ch04 Acids Bases and pH - Worksheet]] · Theory: [[Ch04 Acids Bases and pH]] · Wrong? → [[Mistakes - Chemistry]]

## Level 1
1. pH 3, and pH 8.
2. Two pH units means a factor of $10^2 = 100$. pH 5.5 has 100 times more H⁺.
3. pH = 14 − 4 = 10
4. HCO₃⁻, and NH₄⁺

## Level 2
5. pH = p$K_a$, so $\log(\text{ratio}) = 0$ and the ratio is 1 : 1.
6. 7.0 + log 10 = 8.0
7. pH < p$K_a$, so the group is mostly protonated. [A⁻]/[HA] $= 10^{7.4-9.4} = 10^{-2}$, so there are 100 protonated for every 1 deprotonated.
8. Both carboxyl groups are deprotonated (−1 each, −2 in total) and the amino group is protonated (+1). The net charge is **−1**.
9. HEPES: its p$K_a$ (7.5) is closest to 7.4, so its buffer capacity is highest there. Tris also works at 7.4, but it is near the edge of its range and is temperature-sensitive. Acetate is useless at 7.4.

## Level 3
10. $0.03\cdot40 = 1.2$; $24/1.2 = 20$; $\log_{10}20 = \log_{10}2 + 1 = 1.3$, so pH = 6.1 + 1.3 = **7.4**.
11. $0.03\cdot20 = 0.6$; $24/0.6 = 40$; $\log_{10}40 = 1.6$, so pH ≈ **7.7**. This is **respiratory alkalosis**.
12. Low CO₂ causes cerebral **vasoconstriction**, so blood flow falls (dizziness, tingling). Alkalosis increases neuronal **excitability** (fewer protons block NMDA receptors, and free Ca²⁺ falls because more of it binds to albumin, which makes nerves more excitable, as in tetany).

## Level 4
13. Fraction deprotonated $= \frac{1}{1 + 10^{\text{p}K_a - \text{pH}}}$.
    - Vesicle (pH 5): $\frac{1}{1 + 10^{2}} = \frac{1}{101}\approx1\%$.
    - After fusion (pH 7): $\frac{1}{1+1} = 50\%$.
    - The signal increases about **50-fold**, so fusion events "light up".
14. In acidic tissue, more lidocaine is in the charged form BH⁺, so less uncharged base is available to cross the nerve membrane and reach its binding site inside the Na⁺ channel.
15. Hyperventilation lowers pCO₂, which causes alkalosis and increases neuronal excitability. It also reduces cerebral blood flow. Together these promote synchronised thalamocortical spike-wave discharges, which is why it is used as a provocation method.
16. $10^{7.4-7.9} = 10^{-0.5}\approx0.316$. The fraction of B is $\frac{0.316}{1.316}\approx0.24$, i.e. about **24 %** uncharged.
