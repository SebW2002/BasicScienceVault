# Ch03 Intermolecular Forces and Solubility – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Ch03 Intermolecular Forces and Solubility]] · Solutions: [[Ch03 Intermolecular Forces and Solubility - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Chemistry]]
> No calculator needed.

## Level 1 – Recall
1. Classify as hydrophilic, hydrophobic or amphiphilic: glucose, a fatty acid tail, a phospholipid, Na⁺, O₂, cholesterol, SDS.
2. How are water molecules oriented around a K⁺ ion and around a Cl⁻ ion?
3. What forms in water from a) a single-tailed detergent, b) a two-tailed phospholipid?

## Level 2 – Understanding
4. Why does table salt dissolve in water but not in oil?
5. Mg²⁺ and Na⁺ are about the same size, but Mg²⁺ holds its hydration shell much more tightly. Why?
6. Explain why phospholipid bilayers form spontaneously, although no covalent bonds are formed.
7. What does $\log P = 3$ mean? What does $\log P = -1$ mean?

## Level 3 – Apply
8. Substance A has $\log P = 2$, substance B has $\log P = 0$. How many times more lipophilic is A than B?
9. Predict which crosses the BBB by simple diffusion: a) ethanol b) glucose c) dopamine d) caffeine e) Na⁺. For those that cannot, name how (or whether) they get into the brain.
10. Two drugs bind the same receptor equally well. Drug X has many –OH and –COOH groups; drug Y has methyl groups in their place. Which is the better candidate for a CNS drug? What could be a disadvantage if a molecule is extremely lipophilic?

## Level 4 – Neuro transfer
11. Heroin (diacetylmorphine) acts faster and more strongly than morphine although it is converted to morphine in the brain. Explain.
12. Why is L-DOPA, and not dopamine itself, given to Parkinson's patients?
13. For immunofluorescence, cells are treated with Triton X-100 after fixation. Why is this needed to stain an intracellular protein?
14. Why does a very lipid-rich structure like myelin act as an electrical insulator?
