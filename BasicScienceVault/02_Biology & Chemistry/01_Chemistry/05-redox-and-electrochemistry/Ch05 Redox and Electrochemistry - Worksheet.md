# Ch05 Redox and Electrochemistry – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Ch05 Redox and Electrochemistry]] · Solutions: [[Ch05 Redox and Electrochemistry - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Chemistry]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮. Use $61.5\,\text{mV}$ for $2.303RT/F$; $\log_{10}2\approx0.3$, $\log_{10}5\approx0.7$.

## Level 1 – Concepts
1. Define the electrochemical gradient in one sentence.
2. What is happening at the equilibrium potential of an ion?
3. Oxidation or reduction? a) NADH → NAD⁺ b) O₂ → H₂O c) Fe³⁺ → Fe²⁺
4. What is the final electron acceptor of the mitochondrial electron transport chain? Where is the ATP synthase located?

## Level 2 – Nernst
5. Na⁺: inside 15 mM, outside 150 mM. Compute $E_\text{Na}$.
6. K⁺: inside 160 mM, outside 4 mM. Compute $E_\text{K}$.
7. Cl⁻: inside 12 mM, outside 120 mM. Compute $E_\text{Cl}$ (careful with $z$!).
8. Ca²⁺: inside 0.0002 mM, outside 2 mM. Compute $E_\text{Ca}$.
9. Extracellular K⁺ rises from 4 mM to 40 mM (inside 160 mM). By how much does $E_\text{K}$ change? Which direction?

## Level 3 – Driving force & GHK
10. At $V_m = -70\,\text{mV}$, with $E_\text{Na} = +60$, $E_\text{K} = -90$, $E_\text{Cl} = -70$ mV: compute the driving force $V_m - E$ for each ion. Which way does each flow if its channels open?
11. A GABA_A synapse with $E_\text{Cl} = -70\,\text{mV}$ opens while the cell sits at −70 mV. Is there a current? Does the synapse still have an effect?
12. Simplified GHK (ignore Cl⁻): $[\text{K}^+]_i = 100$, $[\text{K}^+]_o = 4$, $[\text{Na}^+]_i = 10$, $[\text{Na}^+]_o = 100$ mM.
    a) Compute $V_m$ for $P_\text{Na}/P_\text{K} = 0.06$ (rest).
    b) Compute $V_m$ for $P_\text{Na}/P_\text{K} = 10$ (during an AP).
13. Redox: $\Delta E^{\circ\prime} = 1.14\,\text{V}$ between NADH and O₂, with $n = 2$. Estimate $\Delta G = -nF\Delta E$ (use $F\approx10^5\,\text{C/mol}$). How many ATP (about 50 kJ/mol each under cellular conditions) could this energy theoretically make?

## Level 4 – Neuro transfer
14. Why do immature neurons respond to GABA with depolarisation? Which transporter changes during development?
15. During a stroke, extracellular K⁺ rises sharply. Use the Nernst equation to explain why neurons depolarise. What role do astrocytes normally play here?
16. MPTP (a contaminant of a synthetic heroin) caused severe parkinsonism in young drug users. Explain the mechanism in three steps.
17. 🧮 Compute $V_m$ with the full GHK equation for $P_\text{K}:P_\text{Na}:P_\text{Cl} = 1:0.05:0.45$, K⁺ 140/5, Na⁺ 15/145, Cl⁻ 10/110 (in/out, mM).
