# Ch07 Organic Chemistry Essentials – Solutions

> Worksheet: [[Ch07 Organic Chemistry Essentials - Worksheet]] · Theory: [[Ch07 Organic Chemistry Essentials]] · Wrong? → [[Mistakes - Chemistry]]

## Level 1
1. a) carboxyl b) amine c) thiol (sulfhydryl) d) phosphate e) hydroxyl f) amide (the peptide bond)
2. Carboxyl: −1 (COO⁻). Amine: +1 (NH₃⁺). Phosphate: −1 to −2. Hydroxyl: neutral.
3. Two hydroxyl groups on an aromatic ring (a **catechol**) and a primary **amine** (so dopamine is a cat**echol**-**amine**).
4. An **ester** bond (acetic acid + the alcohol group of choline).

## Level 2
5. Condensation; H₂O is released.
6. Hydrolysis of the ester bond. Products: **acetate** and **choline**. Choline is taken back up into the terminal (by the high-affinity choline transporter) and reused for ACh synthesis.
7. Oxidation: each –SH loses H⁺ + e⁻.
8. Glutamic acid decarboxylase (GAD) removes the **α-carboxyl group as CO₂** (decarboxylation). What remains is γ-aminobutyric acid. Interesting: the main excitatory transmitter is converted into the main inhibitory one in one step.
9. The **–OH group** of the serine side chain is esterified with phosphate. The protein gains 1–2 negative charges, which can change its conformation and its interactions (it acts as a switch).

## Level 3
10. It must carry **four different substituents**.
11. **Glycine**: its side chain is just an H atom, so the α-carbon carries two identical H atoms.
12. Cis/trans isomers differ in arrangement around a double bond (or ring) and are **not** mirror images. They have different physical properties. Enantiomers are non-superimposable **mirror images** and have identical physical properties in an achiral environment.
13. Receptors, enzymes and transporters are chiral themselves. Two mirror images make different contacts with them, just as a right hand fits a right glove but not a left one. This changes affinity and efficacy.

## Level 4
14. ACh has a **quaternary** ammonium group (N with 4 carbon substituents), which is **permanently** positively charged and therefore can't diffuse through lipid membranes. Nicotine's tertiary amine is in equilibrium between a charged and an uncharged form. The uncharged fraction is lipophilic and crosses the BBB rapidly.
15. Esketamine (S-ketamine) has about a 3–4 times higher NMDA affinity than R-ketamine, so the same effect needs a lower dose. Only L-DOPA is a substrate of the transporter (LAT1) and of aromatic L-amino-acid decarboxylase, so D-DOPA would be useless and would only add side effects.
16. Rotation around a double bond is normally blocked, so both forms are stable. Light energy briefly breaks the π bond, and the molecule snaps into the other form. The large change in shape (bent → straight) forces a conformational change in the opsin protein, which activates the G protein transducin: a sensitive, one-photon trigger.
17. **Esters** are rapidly hydrolysed by plasma esterases (pseudocholinesterase). **Amides** are much more stable and must be broken down slowly in the liver, so their action lasts longer.
