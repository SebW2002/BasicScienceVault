# Ch08 Biomolecules – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Ch08 Biomolecules]] · Solutions: [[Ch08 Biomolecules - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Chemistry]]
> No calculator needed.

## Level 1 – Recall
1. Name the four classes of biomolecules and one example of each that is important in the brain.
2. What are the three components of a nucleotide? What is the difference between DNA and RNA nucleotides?
3. Which amino acids are negatively charged at pH 7.4? Which are positively charged?
4. Name three neurotransmitters that are amino acids, and three that are made from amino acids (with their precursor).

## Level 2 – Structure
5. Draw (or describe) how two amino acids form a dipeptide. What is released? Why is the resulting bond planar?
6. What is the difference between saturated and unsaturated fatty acids, and how does this affect membrane fluidity?
7. Which bonds link the nucleotides within a DNA strand, and which link the two strands?
8. What is the difference between N- and O-linked glycosylation (amino acid, compartment)?

## Level 3 – Apply
9. How many different tripeptides can be built from the 20 standard amino acids? How many DNA sequences of length 10 are there?
10. A DNA strand has the sequence 5'-ATGCCGTA-3'. Write the complementary strand with its direction. How many hydrogen bonds hold the double strand together?
11. Why does the hydrolysis of ATP release energy that the cell can use? Name two processes in a neuron that consume ATP.

## Level 4 – Neuro transfer
12. Why must the brain make its own cholesterol? Which gene is the strongest common genetic risk factor for late-onset Alzheimer's disease, and what does its protein do?
13. Why does caffeine keep you awake? (Hint: adenosine.)
14. In PKU, the enzyme phenylalanine hydroxylase is defective. Which amino acid becomes deficient, and which transmitter synthesis pathway can be affected?
15. Perineuronal nets are made of glycosylated proteoglycans. Enzymatically digesting them in adult animals can reopen critical periods for visual plasticity. What does this suggest about the role of the extracellular glyco-matrix?
