# Ch06 Solutions and Concentration – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Ch06 Solutions and Concentration]] · Solutions: [[Ch06 Solutions and Concentration - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Chemistry]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮. Molar masses: glucose 180 g/mol, NaCl 58.5 g/mol, KCl 74.5 g/mol.

## Level 1 – Units & molarity
1. Convert: 0.25 M to mM; 50 µM to mM; 2 nM to µM.
2. 9 g glucose are dissolved in 500 mL. What is the molarity?
3. How many grams of NaCl do you need for 1 L of 100 mM NaCl?
4. What is the osmolarity of a) 10 mM glucose, b) 150 mM NaCl, c) 2 mM CaCl₂ (ideal, complete dissociation)?

## Level 2 – Dilutions
5. You have a 10 mM stock of a drug. How much do you need for 50 mL of 10 µM working solution?
6. A 1:1000 dilution of a 5 mM stock gives which concentration?
7. Serial dilution: 1 mM, then 1:10, then 1:10 again. Final concentration?

## Level 3 – Osmosis & diffusion
8. A cell (internal 290 mOsm/L) is placed in a) 150 mOsm/L, b) 290 mOsm/L, c) 500 mOsm/L. What happens in each case?
9. Using $t = \frac{x^2}{2D}$ with $D = 0.5\,\mu\text{m}^2/\text{ms}$ for glutamate: how long does it take to cross a 20 nm cleft? To diffuse 1 µm?
10. With $D = 1\,\mu\text{m}^2/\text{ms}$, how long would diffusion over 1 cm take (in hours, roughly)? What does this mean for proteins made in the soma but needed at the synapse 1 m away?
11. If the distance doubles, how does the diffusion time change? If $D$ doubles?

## Level 4 – Neuro transfer
12. **aCSF:** compute the approximate osmolarity of NaCl 125, KCl 2.5, NaHCO₃ 26, NaH₂PO₄ 1.25, CaCl₂ 2, MgCl₂ 1, glucose 25 (mM), assuming complete dissociation.
13. **Oedema:** why does intravenous mannitol reduce brain swelling? Why would it not work if the BBB were completely broken down?
14. **Hyponatraemia:** why must chronic low plasma Na⁺ be corrected slowly?
15. 🧮 **Osmotic pressure:** using $\pi = cRT$ ($R = 8.314$, $T = 310\,\text{K}$, $c$ in mol/m³), compute $\pi$ for a gradient of 1 mOsm/L (= 1 mol/m³) in Pa and in mmHg (1 mmHg ≈ 133 Pa).
