> [[MOC-chemistry|↑ Chemistry MOC]] · [[MOC-neuroscience-foundations|Neuroscience Foundations]] · Template: [[Mistake Entry Template]]

In biology and chemistry, many "mistakes" are **misconceptions**: plausible-sounding but wrong mental models. Log them here, together with the correct model and a question that tests it.

## Classic misconceptions and calculation traps (pre-filled)
| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Check / why | Topic |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Na⁺ is smaller, so it passes K⁺ channels more easily" | the K⁺ filter replaces K⁺'s hydration shell; Na⁺ doesn't fit and stays hydrated | dehydration energy | [[Ch01 Atomic Structure]] |
| "An action potential changes ion concentrations noticeably" | only ~10⁻⁴ of the ions move; it's charge at the membrane, not bulk concentration | $Q = CV$ | [[Ch01 Atomic Structure]] |
| "The hydrophobic effect is an attraction between oil molecules" | it's driven by water entropy | – | [[Ch02 Chemical Bonds]] |
| "Hydrogen bonds are bonds to hydrogen atoms" (any C–H) | donor must be N–H/O–H (F–H), acceptor N/O | C–H is not a donor | [[Ch02 Chemical Bonds]] |
| "Ionic bonds are always strong" | in water ($\varepsilon = 80$) they are weak | – | [[Ch02 Chemical Bonds]] |
| "Polar bonds ⇒ polar molecule" | geometry can cancel dipoles (CO₂) | vector sum | [[Ch03 Intermolecular Forces and Solubility]] |
| "pH 6 is twice as acidic as pH 7" | 10 times more H⁺ | log scale | [[Ch04 Acids Bases and pH]] |
| Henderson–Hasselbalch ratio upside down | pH = p$K_a$ + log(**base**/**acid**) | pH > p$K_a$ ⇒ more base | [[Ch04 Acids Bases and pH]] |
| "Below its p$K_a$, an amine is uncharged" | below p$K_a$ it is **protonated** (–NH₃⁺) | – | [[Ch04 Acids Bases and pH]] |
| Nernst: ratio in/out instead of out/in | $E = \frac{61.5}{z}\log\frac{c_\text{out}}{c_\text{in}}$ | $E_\text{K}$ must be negative | [[Ch05 Redox and Electrochemistry]] |
| Forgot $z$ (Ca²⁺: 2, Cl⁻: −1) | divide by $z$ incl. sign | $E_\text{Cl}$ negative | [[Ch05 Redox and Electrochemistry]] |
| GHK: Cl⁻ on the same side as cations | Cl⁻ **inside** in numerator | negative charge | [[Ch05 Redox and Electrochemistry]] |
| "The Na⁺/K⁺ pump directly creates the resting potential" | it maintains the gradients; $V_m$ comes mainly from K⁺ leak (pump adds only a few mV directly) | – | [[Ch05 Redox and Electrochemistry]] |
| "At $E_\text{ion}$, no ions move" | equal flux in both directions; **net** flux zero | – | [[Ch05 Redox and Electrochemistry]] |
| "ATP energy is stored in a high-energy bond that releases energy when broken" | breaking bonds costs energy; the products are more stable, and cells keep ATP far from equilibrium | ΔG of the whole reaction | [[Ch08 Biomolecules]] |
| NaCl 150 mM = 150 mOsm/L | ≈ 300 mOsm/L (2 particles) | count ions | [[Ch06 Solutions and Concentration]] |
| "Water flows towards lower concentration" | water flows towards higher **solute** concentration (osmolarity) | – | [[Ch06 Solutions and Concentration]] |
| diffusion time ∝ distance | ∝ distance² | $t = x^2/2D$ | [[Ch06 Solutions and Concentration]] |
| $c_1V_1 = c_2V_2$ with mixed units | convert mM/µM and mL/µL first | – | [[Ch06 Solutions and Concentration]] |
| "Enantiomers are chemically identical, so same drug effect" | chiral receptors distinguish them | esketamine, L-DOPA | [[Ch07 Organic Chemistry Essentials]] |
| "Glycine is chiral like all amino acids" | glycine is achiral (R = H) | – | [[Ch07 Organic Chemistry Essentials]] |
| DNA strands written in the same direction | antiparallel: 5'→3' pairs with 3'→5' | – | [[Ch08 Biomolecules]] |
