# Bio04 Nucleic Acids and Gene Expression – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Bio04 Nucleic Acids and Gene Expression]] · Solutions: [[Bio04 Nucleic Acids and Gene Expression - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Biology]]
> No calculator needed. Codons used: AUG = Met (start), UUU = Phe, GGC = Gly, AAA = Lys, UGG = Trp, UAA/UAG/UGA = stop.

## Level 1 – Recall
1. State the central dogma and name the enzyme for each step.
2. What are the three processing steps of eukaryotic pre-mRNA?
3. What does "semiconservative replication" mean?
4. Name two epigenetic mechanisms, one activating and one repressing.

## Level 2 – Mechanics
5. Template DNA strand: 3'-TAC AAA CCG TTT ATC-5'. Write the mRNA (5' → 3') and translate it.
6. Classify both mutations: a) in the mRNA from task 5, the second codon changes from UUU to UUC (also Phe); b) in another gene, a UGG (Trp) codon in the middle of the coding sequence becomes UGA.
7. A single base is deleted right after the start codon. What happens to the protein?
8. Why does DNA polymerase need a primer, and why is the lagging strand synthesised in fragments?

## Level 3 – Combinatorics & regulation
9. A gene has three alternative exon clusters with 4, 3 and 2 mutually exclusive choices. How many isoforms are possible?
10. Explain how a CREB-dependent chain of events links synaptic activity to new protein synthesis. Why is c-Fos used as an activity marker?
11. HDAC inhibitors in animal models enhance memory consolidation. Propose a mechanism.

## Level 4 – Neuro transfer
12. Huntington's disease is caused by a CAG repeat expansion. Which amino acid is repeated in the protein? Why doesn't the reading frame shift downstream?
13. Explain how nusinersen treats spinal muscular atrophy. Why does targeting splicing work here and not, for example, in a complete gene deletion?
14. Neurons don't divide. Why do they still need DNA repair, and why could defective repair be particularly damaging for them?
15. Two neurons from the same brain have the same genome but very different electrical properties. Name two levels of gene regulation that could explain this.
