# Bio02 Membranes and Transport – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Bio02 Membranes and Transport]] · Solutions: [[Bio02 Membranes and Transport - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Biology]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮.

## Level 1 – Recall
1. Which of these cross a pure lipid bilayer easily: O₂, Na⁺, glucose, CO₂, ethanol, glutamate?
2. Give the stoichiometry of the Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase.
3. Name one example each of a voltage-gated, a ligand-gated and a mechanosensitive channel.
4. Name three factors that increase membrane fluidity.

## Level 2 – Understanding
5. Compare channels and carriers: speed, saturation, conformational change.
6. Why is the Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase called "electrogenic"?
7. Explain how the Na⁺ gradient can be used to transport glutamate *into* astrocytes against its concentration gradient.
8. Which part of a voltage-gated channel senses voltage, and how?
9. Phosphatidylserine normally sits on the inner leaflet. What does it signal when exposed on the outside?

## Level 3 – Calculation
10. A channel has $\gamma = 20\,\text{pS}$. Compute the single-channel current at $V = -80\,\text{mV}$ if $E_\text{rev} = 0\,\text{mV}$. Inward or outward?
11. A cell has 1000 such channels, each with $P_\text{open} = 0.1$. Mean macroscopic current at −80 mV?
12. A spherical soma has a membrane area of $10^{-5}\,\text{cm}^2$ ($\approx 1000\,\mu\text{m}^2$). With $c_m = 1\,\mu\text{F/cm}^2$: total capacitance? With $R_m = 100\,\text{M}\Omega$: membrane time constant $\tau = RC$?

## Level 4 – Neuro transfer
13. Cocaine and amphetamine both increase synaptic dopamine but act differently on DAT. Explain both mechanisms.
14. Loss-of-function mutations in Nav1.1 cause epilepsy, although fewer Na⁺ channels should make neurons *less* excitable. Explain the paradox (Dravet syndrome).
15. What happens to a neuron's resting potential and gradients over minutes to hours if the Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase is blocked with ouabain?
16. 🧮 A Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase cycles at about 100 Hz. How many pumps would be needed to export the ~$6\times10^7$ Na⁺ ions that enter during one action potential within 1 s?
