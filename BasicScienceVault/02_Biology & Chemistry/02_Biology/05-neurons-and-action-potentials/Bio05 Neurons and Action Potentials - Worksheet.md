# Bio05 Neurons and Action Potentials – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Bio05 Neurons and Action Potentials]] · Solutions: [[Bio05 Neurons and Action Potentials - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Biology]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮.

## Level 1 – Recall
1. Name the parts of a neuron in the order in which information flows.
2. Which cells form myelin in the CNS and in the PNS? What is the key difference?
3. Name the phases of an action potential and the main ion current in each.
4. What is the difference between the absolute and the relative refractory period?

## Level 2 – Understanding
5. Why is the resting potential close to $E_\text{K}$ and not to $E_\text{Na}$?
6. Explain the positive feedback in the rising phase. What ends it?
7. Why is the AP initiated at the axon initial segment and not in the dendrites?
8. Why does an AP normally travel only in one direction along the axon?
9. What would happen to the AP if a) TTX, b) TEA were applied?

## Level 3 – Quantitative
10. A myelinated axon has a diameter of 10 µm. Estimate its conduction velocity (6 m/s per µm). How long does an AP take to travel 1 m?
11. An unmyelinated C fibre conducts at 1 m/s. How long does a pain signal from the foot (1 m) take? Compare with task 10.
12. The length constant of an axon is $\lambda = 1\,\text{mm}$. What fraction of a passive voltage change remains after 1 mm? After 2 mm? (Use $e^{-1}\approx0.37$.)
13. If the membrane resistance $r_m$ is increased fourfold by myelin, how does $\lambda$ change?
14. An unmyelinated axon's velocity scales with $\sqrt d$. How much thicker must it become to conduct twice as fast?

## Level 4 – Neuro transfer
15. In multiple sclerosis, visual evoked potentials show delayed latencies. Explain this at the level of the axon.
16. Fast-spiking PV interneurons can fire at > 200 Hz. Which properties of their channels make this possible?
17. 🧮 A neuron with $\tau = 20\,\text{ms}$ receives a constant current that would depolarise it by $V_\infty = 20\,\text{mV}$ above rest. Threshold is 15 mV above rest. Using $t = -\tau\ln(1 - V_\theta/V_\infty)$, when does it reach threshold?
