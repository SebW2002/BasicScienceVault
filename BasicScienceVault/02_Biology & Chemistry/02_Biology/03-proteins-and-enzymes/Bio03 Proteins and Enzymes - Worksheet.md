# Bio03 Proteins and Enzymes – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Bio03 Proteins and Enzymes]] · Solutions: [[Bio03 Proteins and Enzymes - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Biology]]
> No calculator needed.

## Level 1 – Recall
1. Name the four levels of protein structure and the main interactions at each level.
2. Which protein aggregates in a) Alzheimer's disease (two), b) Parkinson's disease, c) Huntington's disease, d) prion diseases?
3. Which G protein (Gs, Gi, Gq) couples to: D1, D2, µ-opioid, mGluR5, β-adrenergic receptor?
4. What is the difference between an agonist, an antagonist and a partial agonist?

## Level 2 – Understanding
5. Why are transmembrane segments of channels usually α-helices of about 20 hydrophobic amino acids? (Use 0.15 nm rise per residue.)
6. Describe the GPCR signalling cycle from ligand binding to switch-off in five steps.
7. Explain why prion diseases are "infectious" although no nucleic acid is involved.
8. What do $K_m$ and $V_\text{max}$ mean? How does a competitive inhibitor change them? A non-competitive one?

## Level 3 – Calculation
9. Michaelis–Menten: compute $v/V_\text{max}$ for $[S] = K_m$, $[S] = 3K_m$, $[S] = K_m/4$ and $[S] = 99K_m$.
10. A receptor has $K_d = 2\,\text{nM}$. Which ligand concentration gives 50 % occupancy? 90 %?
11. A competitive inhibitor at $[I] = K_i$ is present. By what factor does the apparent $K_m$ change? What substrate concentration now gives half-maximal rate?
12. Hill equation with $n = 4$ (like the four Ca²⁺ binding sites on a sensor): compute θ for $[L] = K/2$, $K$, $2K$. Compare with $n = 1$. What does high cooperativity achieve?

## Level 4 – Neuro transfer
13. Donepezil, used in Alzheimer's disease, is a reversible AChE inhibitor. Why does inhibiting an enzyme improve cholinergic transmission?
14. Why do GPCR-mediated effects typically take longer (100 ms to s) but last longer than ionotropic effects (ms)?
15. Buprenorphine is a partial agonist at µ-opioid receptors. Why does it cause less respiratory depression than full agonists, and why can it trigger withdrawal in someone taking heroin?
16. Activation of D2 receptors on striatal neurons reduces cAMP and opens GIRK channels. Predict the effect on the excitability of these neurons.
