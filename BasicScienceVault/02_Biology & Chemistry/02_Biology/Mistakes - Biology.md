## Classic misconceptions (pre-filled)
| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Check / why | Topic |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Kinesin moves towards the soma" | kinesin → plus end (anterograde, to the terminal); dynein → minus end (retrograde) | axon MTs: plus-end out | [[Bio01 Cell Biology]] |
| "Botox and tetanus toxin do the same thing" | botulinum: NMJ, flaccid; tetanus: inhibitory interneurons, spastic | where the toxin acts | [[Bio01 Cell Biology]] |
| "Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase pumps 3 K⁺ in and 2 Na⁺ out" | **3 Na⁺ out, 2 K⁺ in** | "3 salty out" | [[Bio02 Membranes and Transport]] |
| "Channels use ATP" | channels are passive; pumps use ATP; secondary transporters use gradients | – | [[Bio02 Membranes and Transport]] |
| "Fewer Na⁺ channels always means less excitability" | network effects: loss in interneurons → disinhibition (Dravet) | cell type matters | [[Bio02 Membranes and Transport]] |
| "Competitive inhibitors lower $V_\text{max}$" | competitive: $K_m$ ↑; non-competitive: $V_\text{max}$ ↓ | more substrate wins against a competitive inhibitor | [[Bio03 Proteins and Enzymes]] |
| "Low $K_m$ / $K_d$ = low affinity" | low $K_m$/$K_d$ = **high** affinity | half-saturation at lower [S] | [[Bio03 Proteins and Enzymes]] |
| "Gi activates adenylyl cyclase" | Gs ↑ cAMP, Gi ↓ cAMP, Gq → PLC → IP₃/DAG | – | [[Bio03 Proteins and Enzymes]] |
| "Prions contain nucleic acid" | protein-only template conversion | – | [[Bio03 Proteins and Enzymes]] |
| "Epigenetic = changes in DNA sequence" | changes in gene activity **without** sequence change | – | [[Bio04 Nucleic Acids and Gene Expression]] |
| "mRNA reads the coding strand" | RNA pol reads the **template** strand; mRNA = coding strand with U | – | [[Bio04 Nucleic Acids and Gene Expression]] |
| "One gene = one protein" | alternative splicing → many isoforms | neurexins | [[Bio04 Nucleic Acids and Gene Expression]] |
| "Resting potential is because the pump moves charge" | mainly K⁺ leak + gradients | – | [[Bio05 Neurons and Action Potentials]] |
| "Repolarisation happens because Na⁺ flows out" | Nav inactivation + K⁺ efflux | – | [[Bio05 Neurons and Action Potentials]] |
| "Stronger stimulus → bigger AP" | AP is all-or-none; stronger stimulus → **higher rate** | – | [[Bio05 Neurons and Action Potentials]] |
| "Myelin covers the nodes" | nodes of Ranvier are **gaps** with dense Nav channels | – | [[Bio05 Neurons and Action Potentials]] |
| "Refractory period comes from K⁺ channels only" | absolute: Nav inactivation; relative: + K⁺ | – | [[Bio05 Neurons and Action Potentials]] |
| "GABA is always inhibitory" | depolarising in immature neurons (high Cl⁻) | $E_\text{Cl}$ vs $V_m$ | [[Bio06 Synapses and Neurotransmitters]] |
| "A transmitter is excitatory or inhibitory" | the **receptor** (and $E_\text{rev}$) decides; e.g. glutamate on mGluR2/3 is inhibitory | – | [[Bio06 Synapses and Neurotransmitters]] |
| "NMDA receptors open with glutamate alone" | glutamate + glycine/D-serine + depolarisation (Mg²⁺ unblock) | – | [[Bio06 Synapses and Neurotransmitters]] |
| "LTD = forgetting / reversal of LTP only" | its own mechanism (moderate Ca²⁺, phosphatases, AMPA endocytosis) | – | [[Bio06 Synapses and Neurotransmitters]] |
| "Indirect pathway = less movement because dopamine excites it" | dopamine **inhibits** indirect (D2) and excites direct (D1) | – | [[Bio07 Neural Circuits and Systems]] |
| "Microglia are neural glia" | yolk-sac macrophage origin | – | [[Bio07 Neural Circuits and Systems]] |
| "Glia outnumber neurons 10:1" | ~1:1 in human brain | – | [[Bio07 Neural Circuits and Systems]] |
| "Dorsal always means up" | in human forebrain dorsal = superior, but in spinal cord = posterior | neuraxis bends | [[Bio07 Neural Circuits and Systems]] |
