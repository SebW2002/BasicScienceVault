|---|---|---|---|
| "Heavier objects fall faster" | without air resistance, all fall equally fast | $v = \sqrt{2gh}$, mass cancels | [[Ph01 Mechanics and Energy]] |
| "Energy is used up" | energy is converted (often into heat), never destroyed | first law | [[Ph01 Mechanics and Energy]] |
| "Inertia matters for molecules" | low Reynolds number: viscosity dominates | – | [[Ph01 Mechanics and Energy]] |
| "Current and voltage are the same thing" | voltage = driving force between two points; current = charge flow through | water: pressure vs. flow | [[Ph02 Electricity and Charge]] |

| "Electrons carry current in neurons" | ions do; electrons only in metal wires and electrodes | – | [[Ph02 Electricity and Charge]] |
| Forgot $\varepsilon_r$ in Coulomb's law | water reduces forces ~80-fold | – | [[Ph02 Electricity and Charge]] |
| "The membrane potential exists throughout the cytoplasm as excess charge" | excess charge only in a ~nm layer at the membrane; bulk is neutral | – | [[Ph03 Electric Fields and Potential]] |
| "−70 mV is a small voltage, so the field is small" | 14 MV/m across 5 nm | $E = V/d$ | [[Ph03 Electric Fields and Potential]] |
| "At equilibrium, no ions move" | equal flux in both directions | – | [[Ph03 Electric Fields and Potential]] |
| Parallel resistances added | in parallel, **conductances** add | – | [[Ph04 Circuits and Resistance]] |
| "Opening channels increases input resistance" | more open channels = more conductance = **lower** resistance | – | [[Ph04 Circuits and Resistance]] |

| "Bigger cells have a longer τ" | $\tau = R_mc_m$ is independent of size | area cancels | [[Ph05 Capacitance and RC Circuits]] |
| "A strong static magnet induces current" | only **changing** flux induces | $\mathcal E = -d\Phi/dt$ | [[Ph06 Magnetism and Induction]] |
| "TMS stimulates with magnetism directly" | it's the **induced electric field** that depolarises neurons | – | [[Ph06 Magnetism and Induction]] |
| "MEG sees all dipoles" | radial dipoles are silent | – | [[Ph06 Magnetism and Induction]] |
| diffusion distance ∝ time | ∝ √time | $x = \sqrt{2Dt}$ | [[Ph07 Diffusion and Transport]] |
| "Molecules diffuse because they are pushed by the gradient" | random walk; net flux is statistical | entropy | [[Ph07 Diffusion and Transport]] |
| "D ∝ 1/mass" | Stokes–Einstein: $D\propto1/r\propto M^{-1/3}$ | – | [[Ph07 Diffusion and Transport]] |
| "$\Delta G < 0$ means fast" | it means **possible**; speed depends on the barrier | – | [[Ph08 Thermodynamics and Statistics]] |
| using °C instead of K in $kT$, $RT$ | always kelvin | – | [[Ph08 Thermodynamics and Statistics]] |
| "Boltzmann: higher energy state more likely" | $p\propto e^{-E/kT}$: lower energy more likely | – | [[Ph08 Thermodynamics and Statistics]] |
| "Power ∝ amplitude" | power ∝ amplitude² | – | [[Ph09 Waves and Oscillations]] |
| "Sampling at 2× the frequency of interest is enough" | > 2× the **highest** frequency present; anti-alias filter | aliasing | [[Ph09 Waves and Oscillations]] |
| "Membrane = high-pass filter" | passive membrane = **low-pass** ($f_c = 1/2\pi\tau$) | – | [[Ph09 Waves and Oscillations]] |
| "λ ∝ diameter" | $\lambda\propto\sqrt d$ | – | [[Ph10 Signal Propagation and Cable Theory]] |
| "Myelinated velocity ∝ √d" | myelinated ∝ d; unmyelinated ∝ √d | – | [[Ph10 Signal Propagation and Cable Theory]] |
| "APs decay along the axon like EPSPs" | APs regenerate (nonlinear); only passive signals decay | – | [[Ph10 Signal Propagation and Cable Theory]] |
| "More magnification = more detail" | resolution set by λ and NA | – | [[Ph11 Optics and Fluorescence]] |
| "Light bends away from the normal entering water" | towards the normal (higher $n$) | – | [[Ph11 Optics and Fluorescence]] |
| "Emission has shorter wavelength than excitation" | longer (Stokes shift) | energy lost | [[Ph11 Optics and Fluorescence]] |
| "Longer wavelength = more energy" | $E = hc/\lambda$: longer = **less** energy | – | [[Ph12 Quantum Basics for Biophysics]] |
