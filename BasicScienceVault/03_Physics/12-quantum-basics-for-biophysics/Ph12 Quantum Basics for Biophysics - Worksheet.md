# Ph12 Quantum Basics for Biophysics – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Ph12 Quantum Basics for Biophysics]] · Solutions: [[Ph12 Quantum Basics for Biophysics - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Physics]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮. $E[\text{eV}]\approx1240/\lambda[\text{nm}]$; 1 eV ≈ 96.5 kJ/mol; $kT\approx0.027$ eV at 37 °C.

## Level 1 – Photon energy
1. Compute the photon energy (eV) at 620 nm, 496 nm and 1240 nm.
2. Which photon has more energy: blue (450 nm) or red (650 nm)? By roughly what factor?
3. Express the energy of a 470 nm photon in kJ/mol and in units of $kT$.
4. Why can UV light damage DNA directly, but red light cannot?

## Level 2 – Two-photon & photon statistics
5. Which wavelength must two photons have to deliver the same energy as one 460 nm photon?
6. Two-photon excitation probability ∝ $I^2$. If the laser intensity at the focus drops to one-third due to scattering, by what factor does the signal drop?
7. A pixel collects on average 100 photons per frame. What is the shot noise and the SNR? How many photons are needed for SNR = 30?
8. A voltage indicator changes brightness by 2 %. With 10,000 photons per frame, is this change detectable above shot noise (compare the change with $\sqrt N$)?

## Level 3 – Energy levels
9. Order by energy: nuclear spin transition in MRI, vibrational transition, electronic transition.
10. Explain why longer conjugated systems absorb at longer wavelengths.
11. The same retinal absorbs at different wavelengths in different opsins. Why?
12. A DNA solution has absorbance $A = 0.5$ at 260 nm. What fraction of the light passes through?

## Level 4 – Tunnelling & neuro transfer
13. Explain qualitatively why electrons can tunnel between cofactors in mitochondrial complexes, but K⁺ ions cannot tunnel through ion channels.
14. Tunnelling probability decreases by about a factor of 10 for every ~1.7 Å of extra distance in proteins. By what factor does it drop if the distance increases from 10 Å to 15 Å? What does this mean for protein design of the electron transport chain?
15. Why can red-shifted opsins be activated deeper in the brain than ChR2? Give two physical reasons.
16. 🧮 Compute the number of photons per second in a 1 mW blue laser beam at 470 nm ($E_\text{photon} = hc/\lambda$, $h = 6.63\times10^{-34}$ J·s, $c = 3\times10^8$ m/s).
