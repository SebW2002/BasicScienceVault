# Ph10 Signal Propagation and Cable Theory – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Ph10 Signal Propagation and Cable Theory]] · Solutions: [[Ph10 Signal Propagation and Cable Theory - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Physics]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮. $e^{-1}\approx0.37$, $e^{-2}\approx0.14$, $e^{-0.5}\approx0.61$, $\sqrt2\approx1.41$.

## Level 1 – Concepts
1. Name the three electrical properties per unit length of a cable model.
2. What does the length constant λ describe? What fraction of a passive voltage remains after 1 λ and 2 λ?
3. What is the difference between passive spread and active propagation?
4. Why is saltatory conduction faster than continuous conduction?

## Level 2 – Length constant
5. A passive EPSP of 10 mV is generated 0.5 mm from the soma; $\lambda = 0.5$ mm. How large is it at the soma (steady state)? And if λ = 1 mm?
6. Compute λ for $R_m = 20\,\text{k}\Omega\text{·cm}^2$, $R_i = 100\,\Omega\text{·cm}$ and $d = 4\,\mu\text{m}$ (use $\lambda = \sqrt{\frac{R_m d}{4R_i}}$, $d$ in cm).
7. How does λ change if a) the diameter quadruples, b) $R_m$ halves (more open channels)?
8. Compute the electrotonic length of a 1 mm dendrite with λ = 0.5 mm. Is it electrically compact?

## Level 3 – Conduction velocity
9. A myelinated axon has $d = 15$ µm. Estimate its velocity. How long does an AP take for 1 m?
10. An unmyelinated axon conducts at 1 m/s at $d = 1$ µm. How fast would it be at $d = 4$ µm? How thick would it have to be to conduct at 10 m/s?
11. Compare: to reach 30 m/s, how thick must a myelinated vs. an unmyelinated axon be (use the values from tasks 9–10)?
12. Show that the cable equation reduces to the RC equation if there is no spatial voltage gradient.

## Level 4 – Neuro transfer
13. Why are distal inputs on dendrites not only smaller but also slower at the soma?
14. Explain at the level of $R_m$ and $C_m$ why demyelination can block conduction completely.
15. What is the role of the nonlinearity (positive feedback) in the Hodgkin–Huxley model for propagation over long distances?
16. 🧮 A pyramidal cell's apical dendrite has λ = 0.8 mm. An input arrives 1.2 mm from the soma. What fraction reaches the soma passively? Compare with an input at 0.2 mm.
