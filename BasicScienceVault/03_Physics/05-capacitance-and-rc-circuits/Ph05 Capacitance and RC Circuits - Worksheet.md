# Ph05 Capacitance and RC Circuits – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Ph05 Capacitance and RC Circuits]] · Solutions: [[Ph05 Capacitance and RC Circuits - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Physics]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮. $e^{-1}\approx0.37$, $e^{-2}\approx0.14$, $\ln2\approx0.69$.

## Level 1 – Capacitors
1. A 10 µF capacitor is charged to 5 V. How much charge does it hold? How much energy?
2. How does the capacitance of a parallel-plate capacitor change if a) the area doubles, b) the plate distance doubles?
3. A neuron has a membrane area of $2\times10^{-4}$ cm². What is its capacitance ($c_m = 1\,\mu\text{F/cm}^2$)?
4. When does current flow through a capacitor, and when not?

## Level 2 – RC circuit
5. $R = 100\,\text{M}\Omega$, $C = 200\,\text{pF}$. Compute τ.
6. A current step of 100 pA is injected into the cell from task 5. Final voltage change? Voltage after one τ and after two τ?
7. After the current is switched off at 25 mV, how much voltage remains after 40 ms (τ = 20 ms)?
8. How long does it take for a discharging membrane to drop to half its value (τ = 20 ms)?
9. Compute the initial slope $dV/dt$ for $I = 100\,\text{pA}$ and $C = 200\,\text{pF}$ (in mV/ms).

## Level 3 – Membrane properties
10. Show that $\tau_m = R_m\cdot c_m$ doesn't depend on cell size. Compute $\tau_m$ for $R_m = 50\,\text{k}\Omega\text{·cm}^2$.
11. Two neurons have τ = 2 ms and τ = 30 ms. Two EPSPs arrive 10 ms apart. In which neuron do they summate more? Which cell is a coincidence detector?
12. Myelin has 40 membrane layers in series. By what factor does the capacitance change compared with a single membrane?
13. Many synapses open during intense activity and the input resistance halves. What happens to τ?

## Level 4 – Neuron models
14. LIF neuron: $\tau = 20\,\text{ms}$, $V_\text{reset} = 0$, $V_\theta = 15\,\text{mV}$ (relative to rest), and $V_\infty = 30\,\text{mV}$. Time to threshold? (Use $T = \tau\ln\frac{V_\infty - V_\text{reset}}{V_\infty - V_\theta}$.) Firing rate, ignoring refractoriness?
15. What happens in task 14 if $V_\infty = 15\,\text{mV}$? Or 10 mV?
16. 🧮 Exocytosis: a vesicle of 40 nm diameter fuses with the membrane. By how much does the cell capacitance increase? ($A = 4\pi r^2$; $c_m = 1\,\mu\text{F/cm}^2 = 10^{-2}$ F/m².)
