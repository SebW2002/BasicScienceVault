# Ph01 Mechanics and Energy – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Ph01 Mechanics and Energy]] · Solutions: [[Ph01 Mechanics and Energy - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Physics]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮. Use $g\approx10\,\text{m/s}^2$.

## Level 1 – School basics
1. A 2 kg object accelerates at 3 m/s². What force acts on it?
2. A 1.4 kg brain (roughly) sits in a skull. What is its weight in N?
3. You lift a 5 kg box 2 m up. How much work do you do? Where does the energy go?
4. A 0.5 kg ball moves at 4 m/s. What is its kinetic energy?
5. Convert: 1 kcal to kJ; 20 W for 1 hour to J and kJ.

## Level 2 – Energy conservation & power
6. A ball falls from 5 m (no air resistance). Use energy conservation to find its speed at the ground.
7. A spring with $k = 200\,\text{N/m}$ is compressed by 0.1 m. How much energy does it store?
8. A force of 10 N pushes an object 3 m, but at an angle of 60° to the path ($\cos60° = 0.5$). How much work is done?
9. A person climbs 3 m of stairs (70 kg) in 3 s. What is their mechanical power?

## Level 3 – Molecular scale
10. Convert $kT = 4.3\times10^{-21}\,\text{J}$ into pN·nm.
11. Kinesin takes an 8 nm step against a 6 pN load. How much work is done per step (in pN·nm and in multiples of $kT$)?
12. One ATP releases about 50 kJ/mol in the cell. Convert to J per molecule (use $N_A\approx6\times10^{23}$) and to pN·nm. What is kinesin's efficiency from task 11?
13. Explain why a cell doesn't "glide" after a motor stops pulling (keyword: Reynolds number).

## Level 4 – Neuro transfer
14. The brain uses about 20 W. How much energy is that per day in MJ and kcal?
15. 🧮 The brain consumes about 120 g glucose per day. With an energy content of about 15.7 kJ/g, how much power does that correspond to? Compare with 20 W.
16. A hair-cell bundle is deflected by 10 nm against a stiffness of 1 mN/m (= 1 pN/nm). How much energy is stored (in pN·nm and in $kT$)? Why does this have to be compared with $kT$?
