# Ph01 Mechanics and Energy – Solutions

> Worksheet: [[Ph01 Mechanics and Energy - Worksheet]] · Theory: [[Ph01 Mechanics and Energy]] · Wrong? → [[Mistakes - Physics]]

## Level 1
1. $F = 2\cdot3 = 6\,\text{N}$
2. $F = 1.4\cdot10 = 14\,\text{N}$. (In reality the brain floats in CSF, which reduces its effective weight to about 0.5 N.)
3. $W = mgh = 5\cdot10\cdot2 = 100\,\text{J}$. It is stored as potential energy of the box (and your muscles convert extra chemical energy into heat).
4. $\tfrac12\cdot0.5\cdot16 = 4\,\text{J}$
5. 1 kcal ≈ 4.19 kJ; $20\,\text{W}\cdot3600\,\text{s} = 72\,000\,\text{J} = 72\,\text{kJ}$

## Level 2
6. $mgh = \tfrac12mv^2\Rightarrow v = \sqrt{2gh} = \sqrt{100} = 10\,\text{m/s}$ (the mass cancels)
7. $\tfrac12\cdot200\cdot0.01 = 1\,\text{J}$
8. $W = 10\cdot3\cdot0.5 = 15\,\text{J}$
9. $W = 70\cdot10\cdot3 = 2100\,\text{J}$; $P = 2100/3 = 700\,\text{W}$

## Level 3
10. $1\,\text{pN·nm} = 10^{-12}\cdot10^{-9} = 10^{-21}\,\text{J}$, so $kT\approx4.3\,\text{pN·nm}$.
11. $W = 6\cdot8 = 48\,\text{pN·nm}\approx11\,kT$
12. $\frac{5\times10^4}{6\times10^{23}}\approx8.3\times10^{-20}\,\text{J} = 83\,\text{pN·nm}$. Efficiency $\approx48/83\approx58\,\%$.
13. At low Reynolds number, viscous forces dominate over inertia. The momentum of a cell is tiny compared to the drag of the surrounding water, so motion stops almost instantly when the driving force stops.

## Level 4
14. $20\cdot86\,400 = 1.73\times10^6\,\text{J} = 1.73\,\text{MJ}$, and $1730/4.19\approx413\,\text{kcal}$ per day.
15. $120\cdot15.7 = 1884\,\text{kJ}$ per day; $\frac{1.884\times10^6\,\text{J}}{86\,400\,\text{s}}\approx21.8\,\text{W}$, consistent with the ~20 W estimate.
16. $E = \tfrac12kx^2 = \tfrac12\cdot1\cdot100 = 50\,\text{pN·nm}\approx12\,kT$. Thermal motion constantly moves the bundle with energies of about $\tfrac12kT$ per degree of freedom. Signals must be clearly larger than this **thermal noise** to be detected reliably. Hair cells work remarkably close to this physical limit.
