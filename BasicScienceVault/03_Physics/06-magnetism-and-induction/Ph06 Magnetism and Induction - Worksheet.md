# Ph06 Magnetism and Induction – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Ph06 Magnetism and Induction]] · Solutions: [[Ph06 Magnetism and Induction - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Physics]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮. $\mu_0 = 4\pi\times10^{-7}$ T·m/A.

## Level 1 – Basics
1. What creates a magnetic field? What does a changing magnetic field create?
2. Order by strength: MRI scanner, Earth's field, MEG signal, TMS pulse, fridge magnet.
3. Use the right-hand rule: a current flows upward in a vertical wire. In which direction do the field lines circle when viewed from above?
4. State Faraday's law in words. Does a strong but *constant* magnetic field induce a voltage in a stationary loop?

## Level 2 – Calculation
5. A wire carries 10 A. Compute $B$ at 1 cm distance. Compare with the Earth's field.
6. A loop of area 0.01 m² sits in a field that rises from 0 to 1 T in 0.1 ms. Compute the induced EMF.
7. With $E = \frac{r}{2}\frac{dB}{dt}$, compute the induced field at $r = 2$ cm for $dB/dt = 10^4$ T/s.
8. Compute the field of a current of 1 nA at a distance of 1 cm ($B = \mu_0I/2\pi r$). How does this compare with typical MEG signals and the Earth's field?

## Level 3 – Concepts
9. Why does TMS work through the skull, while electrical stimulation through the scalp (with the same aim) requires much higher currents and is painful?
10. Why does a figure-of-eight coil stimulate more focally than a round coil?
11. Explain why MEG is insensitive to radial current dipoles. Which cortical regions does MEG see best?
12. MRI gradients are switched rapidly. Why can this cause tingling in the body, even though the static field is many times stronger?

## Level 4 – Neuro transfer
13. A TMS pulse over left motor cortex produces a twitch in the right hand. Explain the chain from coil current to muscle.
14. Low-frequency (1 Hz) rTMS reduces cortical excitability over time; high-frequency (10 Hz) increases it. Which synaptic plasticity mechanisms could explain this? (Link to [[Bio06 Synapses and Neurotransmitters]].)
15. 🧮 An MEG source is a current dipole of 20 nA·m. Use the simplified estimate $B\approx\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{Q}{r^2}$ to compute the field at $r = 4$ cm. Express the answer in fT.
