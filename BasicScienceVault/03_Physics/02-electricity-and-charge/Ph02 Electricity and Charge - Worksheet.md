# Ph02 Electricity and Charge – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Ph02 Electricity and Charge]] · Solutions: [[Ph02 Electricity and Charge - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Physics]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮. $e\approx1.6\times10^{-19}\,\text{C}$, $k = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\approx9\times10^9\,\text{N·m²/C²}$.

## Level 1 – Basics
1. What is the charge of one Ca²⁺ ion in C? Of one mole of Ca²⁺ ions?
2. A current of 2 A flows for 5 s. How much charge has passed?
3. Explain in your own words the difference between current and voltage. Give the units.
4. Which of the following is a voltage and which a current: −70 mV; 50 pA; 0.5 nA; 20 mV EPSP?

## Level 2 – Coulomb's law
5. Two charges are moved from 1 nm to 3 nm apart. By what factor does the force change?
6. How does the force between two ions change if they move from water ($\varepsilon_r = 80$) into the membrane interior ($\varepsilon_r = 2$)?
7. Compute the force between two elementary charges 1 nm apart in vacuum. Is it attractive or repulsive if both are positive?
8. How does the force change if one ion is Ca²⁺ instead of Na⁺?

## Level 3 – Ions and currents
9. How many monovalent ions per second correspond to a current of 1 pA? Of 1 nA?
10. A single channel conducts 3 pA for 2 ms. How many K⁺ ions pass?
11. Classify as inward or outward current (sign in neurophysiology convention): a) Na⁺ influx b) K⁺ efflux c) Cl⁻ influx d) Ca²⁺ influx. Which of them depolarise?
12. Why can there be a voltage across the membrane but no net current for a particular ion?

## Level 4 – Neuro transfer
13. Explain with Coulomb's law why NaCl dissolves in water but would stay bound in a lipid environment.
14. An Ag/AgCl electrode measures current in a salt solution. Who carries the current in the wire, and who in the solution? What happens at the electrode surface?
15. 🧮 Compute the electrostatic energy (in units of $kT = 4.3\times10^{-21}$ J) of Na⁺ and Cl⁻ at 0.5 nm a) in vacuum, b) in water. Interpret.
