# Ph08 Thermodynamics and Statistics – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Ph08 Thermodynamics and Statistics]] · Solutions: [[Ph08 Thermodynamics and Statistics - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Physics]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮. At 37 °C: $kT = 4.3\times10^{-21}$ J, $RT = 2.6$ kJ/mol, $RT\ln10\approx5.9$ kJ/mol, $kT/e = 26.7$ mV. $e^{-1}\approx0.37$, $e^{-2}\approx0.14$.

## Level 1 – Thermal energy
1. Convert 37 °C and 20 °C to kelvin. By how many % is $kT$ larger at 37 °C?
2. What is the average translational kinetic energy of one molecule at 310 K?
3. Express the energy of a hydrogen bond (20 kJ/mol) and ATP hydrolysis (50 kJ/mol) in units of $RT$.
4. What does $\Delta G < 0$ mean? Does it say how fast a reaction runs?

## Level 2 – Boltzmann
5. Two states differ by $\Delta E = kT$. What is the population ratio? And for $\Delta E = 2kT$, $\Delta E = 2.3kT$?
6. A channel has $V_{1/2} = -30$ mV and $z_g = 4$. Compute the slope factor $s$. Compute $P_\text{open}$ at −30 mV, at $-30 + s$ mV, and at $-30 - 2s$ mV.
7. How does the curve change if $z_g$ doubles?
8. A reaction rate has $Q_{10} = 3$. By what factor does it change from 27 °C to 37 °C? From 17 °C to 37 °C?

## Level 3 – Free energy and equilibrium
9. A reaction has $\Delta G^\circ = -11.8$ kJ/mol at 37 °C. Compute $K$.
10. A drug binds its receptor with $K_d = 1$ nM. Compute $\Delta G^\circ_\text{bind}$ (standard state 1 M, so $K_d = 10^{-9}$). Use $RT\ln10 = 5.9$ kJ/mol.
11. Derive the Nernst equation from $\Delta G = RT\ln\frac{c_\text{in}}{c_\text{out}} + zFV_m = 0$.
12. Compute $\Delta G$ for moving 1 mol of Na⁺ **into** a cell ($c_\text{in} = 15$, $c_\text{out} = 150$ mM, $V_m = -70$ mV, $F\approx96.5$ kJ/(mol·V), $\ln0.1 = -2.3$). Is the process spontaneous?

## Level 4 – Entropy & neuro transfer
13. Four molecules are distributed between two halves of a box. How many arrangements (microstates) correspond to "all on the left" and to "2 left, 2 right"? What does this say about diffusion?
14. Why can't concentration gradients exist forever without energy input? Which process maintains them in neurons?
15. Explain why synaptic release is probabilistic and why it rises steeply with Ca²⁺.
16. 🧮 Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase: compute the $\Delta G$ for pumping 3 Na⁺ out (in 15, out 145 mM) and 2 K⁺ in (in 140, out 5 mM) at $V_m = -70$ mV, 37 °C. Is one ATP (≈ −50 kJ/mol) sufficient?
