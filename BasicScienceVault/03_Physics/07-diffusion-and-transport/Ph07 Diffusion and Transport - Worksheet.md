# Ph07 Diffusion and Transport – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Ph07 Diffusion and Transport]] · Solutions: [[Ph07 Diffusion and Transport - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Physics]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮. 1 µm²/ms = 10⁻⁹ m²/s.

## Level 1 – Concepts
1. Explain why net diffusion from high to low concentration occurs, although each molecule moves randomly.
2. State Fick's first law and explain the minus sign.
3. Using Stokes–Einstein, how does $D$ change if a) temperature rises from 300 K to 310 K, b) the radius doubles, c) viscosity triples?
4. What is osmosis? In which direction does water flow?

## Level 2 – Calculation
5. With $D = 1\,\mu\text{m}^2/\text{ms}$: how long does diffusion over 1 µm, 10 µm and 100 µm take ($t\approx x^2/2D$)?
6. A protein has $D = 0.1\,\mu\text{m}^2/\text{ms}$. How far does it diffuse in 1 s? In 1 min?
7. A concentration falls linearly from 10 mM to 0 mM over 10 µm. Compute the flux for $D = 1\times10^{-9}$ m²/s (watch the units: 1 mM = 1 mol/m³).
8. A Gaussian concentration profile after a point release has width $\sigma = \sqrt{2Dt}$. By what factor is $\sigma$ larger after 4 ms than after 1 ms? By what factor has the peak (in 1D) decreased?

## Level 3 – Electrodiffusion & osmosis
9. Describe the two terms of the Nernst–Planck equation. What follows if the net flux is zero?
10. In the Nernst–Planck equation, why does the drift term contain $c$ (concentration) as a factor?
11. Compute the osmotic pressure of 300 mOsm/L at 310 K ($R = 8.3$ J/(mol·K)) in Pa and in atm (1 atm ≈ 10⁵ Pa).
12. In the brain's extracellular space, the tortuosity is $\lambda_t = 1.6$. By what factor is effective diffusion slowed?

## Level 4 – Neuro transfer
13. Ca²⁺ channels must sit within ~20–50 nm of the Ca²⁺ sensor for fast release. Explain with diffusion and buffering.
14. Why does the apparent diffusion coefficient of water drop in acute stroke? Why is this useful clinically?
15. 🧮 Estimate $D$ for glucose ($r\approx0.4$ nm) in water at 37 °C ($\eta\approx0.7\times10^{-3}$ Pa·s, $kT\approx4.3\times10^{-21}$ J) with Stokes–Einstein. Give the result in µm²/ms.
