# Ph03 Electric Fields and Potential – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Ph03 Electric Fields and Potential]] · Solutions: [[Ph03 Electric Fields and Potential - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Physics]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮. $e = 1.6\times10^{-19}$ C, $F\approx10^5$ C/mol, $kT\approx4.3\times10^{-21}$ J.

## Level 1 – Basics
1. Define the electric field and give its two equivalent units.
2. A charge of $2\times10^{-6}$ C experiences a force of 0.01 N. What is the field strength?
3. What is the difference between potential and potential difference? Which one can you measure?
4. In which direction does a positive ion move in an electric field? A negative one?

## Level 2 – Uniform field
5. Two plates 2 cm apart have a voltage of 10 V between them. Compute the field strength.
6. The membrane is 5 nm thick, and $V_m = -70$ mV. Compute the field strength in V/m and MV/m. Compare with the breakdown field of air (3 MV/m).
7. How much work is needed to move one electron through 1 V? Express in J and eV.
8. How much energy is released when one Na⁺ ion enters a cell at $V_m = -70$ mV (purely electrical part)? Express in J and in units of $kT$.

## Level 3 – Equilibrium potential
9. Describe in four steps how a K⁺-selective membrane develops a negative potential.
10. Why is the net K⁺ flux zero at $E_\text{K}$, although single K⁺ ions still cross?
11. Per mole, the electrical energy of moving K⁺ across −90 mV is $zFV$. Compute it in kJ/mol (use $F\approx96\,500$ C/mol). Compare with the chemical energy $RT\ln(c_o/c_i)$ for $c_o/c_i = 1/30$ at 37 °C ($RT\approx2.6$ kJ/mol, $\ln30\approx3.4$).
12. Why don't the concentrations change noticeably while the membrane potential develops?

## Level 4 – Neuro transfer
13. A voltage sensor moves 3 elementary charges through 50 % of the membrane field when $V_m$ changes from −70 to 0 mV. How much electrical energy is involved (in $kT$)? What does this mean for how steeply channel opening depends on voltage?
14. tDCS creates a field of 0.5 V/m in the cortex. Over a 1 mm long pyramidal cell, what potential difference does this correspond to? Why can this still have effects?
15. 🧮 Compute the potential of a single elementary charge at 1 nm distance in water ($\varepsilon_r = 80$), using $V = \frac{ke}{\varepsilon_rr}$ with $k = 9\times10^9\,\text{N·m²/C²}$, in mV. Compare with the "thermal voltage" $kT/e\approx26.7$ mV.
