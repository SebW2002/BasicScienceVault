# Ph11 Optics and Fluorescence – Worksheet

> Theory: [[Ph11 Optics and Fluorescence]] · Solutions: [[Ph11 Optics and Fluorescence - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Physics]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮. $\sin30° = 0.5$, $\sin45°\approx0.71$, $\sin60°\approx0.87$, $\sin90° = 1$.

## Level 1 – Refraction
1. Light travels at $2.25\times10^8$ m/s in water. Compute the refractive index.
2. A light ray passes from air ($n = 1$) into water ($n = 1.33$) at 30° to the normal. Does it bend towards or away from the normal? Compute $\sin\theta_2$.
3. Compute the critical angle for total internal reflection from glass ($n = 1.5$) to air.
4. Why does brain tissue look white and opaque, although its components are mostly transparent?

## Level 2 – Lenses
5. A lens with $f = 2$ cm images an object at 3 cm. Where is the image? What is the magnification? Is it upright or inverted?
6. An object is placed at 4 cm from the same lens. Image distance and magnification?
7. A microscope has a 60× objective and 10× eyepieces. Total magnification? Does a 100× eyepiece improve resolution?
8. What is the refractive power of a lens with $f = 50$ cm?

## Level 3 – Diffraction & fluorescence
9. Compute the Abbe limit for $\lambda = 600$ nm and NA = 1.2. And with a dry objective of NA = 0.8?
10. Why does a shorter wavelength improve resolution? Why do electron microscopes reach far higher resolution?
11. GFP is excited at 488 nm and emits at 510 nm. What is the Stokes shift? Why is the emission at a longer wavelength?
12. A fluorophore absorbs 1000 photons and emits 600. What is its quantum yield? A second dye has twice the extinction coefficient but $\Phi = 0.3$. Which is brighter?
13. FRET efficiency $E = \frac{1}{1 + (r/R_0)^6}$ with $R_0 = 5$ nm. Compute $E$ for $r = 5$ nm and $r = 10$ nm. What does this make FRET useful for?

## Level 4 – Neuro transfer
14. Why do two-photon microscopes and red-shifted opsins (e.g. ChRmine) reach deeper into the brain? (Scattering scales roughly with $\lambda^{-4}$ for small scatterers: compare 460 nm with 920 nm.)
15. In a long-term calcium imaging session, the fluorescence baseline slowly decreases and cells start to look unhealthy. Name the two effects and three countermeasures.
16. 🧮 An optical fibre has a core with $n_1 = 1.46$ and cladding $n_2 = 1.45$. Compute the critical angle and the numerical aperture $\text{NA} = \sqrt{n_1^2 - n_2^2}$.
