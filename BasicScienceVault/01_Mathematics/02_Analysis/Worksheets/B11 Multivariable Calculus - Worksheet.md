# B11 Multivariable Calculus – Worksheet

> Theory: [[B11 Multivariable Calculus]] · Solutions: [[B11 Multivariable Calculus - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Analysis]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮.

## Level 1 – Partial derivatives
1. Compute $f_x$ and $f_y$ for a) $f = 3x^2y - y^3$ b) $f = e^{xy}$ c) $f = \ln(x^2 + y^2)$.
2. Compute all second derivatives of $f = x^3y^2$ and check that $f_{xy} = f_{yx}$.
3. Compute the gradient of $f(x, y) = x^2 + 4y^2$ at $(1, 1)$. Sketch the contour through this point and the gradient.

## Level 2 – Directional derivatives and critical points
4. For $f$ from task 3, compute the directional derivative at $(1, 1)$ in the direction $\mathbf u = \frac{1}{\sqrt2}(1, 1)$. In which direction is the slope maximal, and how large is it?
5. Find and classify the critical points of $f(x, y) = x^2 + xy + y^2 - 3x$.
6. Find and classify the critical point of $f(x, y) = x^2 - y^2$.
7. Find the critical point of $f(x, y) = -(x - 1)^2 - 2(y + 2)^2 + 5$. Maximum, minimum or saddle?

## Level 3 – Jacobian and chain rule
8. Compute the Jacobian of $\mathbf f(x, y) = (x^2 - y,\ xy)$ at $(1, 2)$.
9. A particle moves along $x(t) = \cos t$, $y(t) = \sin t$ through the field $f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2$. Compute $df/dt$ with the chain rule. Interpret.
10. The system $\dot x = -x + y$, $\dot y = -2y$ has a fixed point at the origin. Compute the Jacobian and its eigenvalues. Stable?

## Level 4 – Integrals and applications
11. Compute $\int_0^1\int_0^2(x + y)\,dy\,dx$.
12. Compute $\iint_Re^{-(x^2 + y^2)}dA$ over the whole plane using polar coordinates. What does this give for $\int_{-\infty}^\infty e^{-x^2}dx$?
13. A place field is $r(x, y) = 20\exp(-(x^2 + y^2)/2)$ Hz (positions in units of 10 cm). Compute $\nabla r$ at $(1, 0)$. In which direction must the animal move to increase the rate fastest?
14. 🧮 Compute the integral of the place field in task 13 over the whole plane, i.e. $\iint r\,dA$ (use polar coordinates).
