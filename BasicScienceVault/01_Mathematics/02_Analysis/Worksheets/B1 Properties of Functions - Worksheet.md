# B1 Properties of Functions – Worksheet

> Theory: [[B1 Properties of Functions]] · Solutions: [[B1 Properties of Functions - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Analysis]]
> No calculator needed.

## Level 1 – Domain & zeros
1. Find the domain: a) $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{x - 2}$ b) $g(x) = \sqrt{x + 3}$ c) $h(x) = \ln(x - 1)$
2. Find all zeros: a) $f(x) = x^2 - 9$ b) $f(x) = x^3 - 4x$ c) $f(x) = e^x - 1$
3. Even, odd or neither? a) $x^4 - x^2$ b) $x^3 + x$ c) $x^2 + x$ d) $\cos x$ e) $x \sin x$

## Level 2 – Rational functions & transformations
4. Find poles and removable discontinuities: a) $\dfrac{x+1}{x^2 - 4}$ b) $\dfrac{x - 1}{x^2 - 1}$
5. Find vertical and horizontal asymptotes of $f(x) = \dfrac{2x + 1}{x - 3}$.
6. Describe how $y = 2(x - 1)^2 + 3$ arises from $y = x^2$. Where is the vertex?
7. For $f(x) = x^2$ and $g(x) = x + 1$, compute $f \circ g$ and $g \circ f$.
8. Find the inverse function: a) $f(x) = 3x - 6$ b) $f(x) = e^{2x}$

## Level 3 – Analysis without derivatives
9. Show that $f(x) = \dfrac{x}{1 + x^2}$ is odd. Find its zeros and horizontal asymptote.
10. On which interval is $f(x) = -2x + 3$ increasing/decreasing? And $f(x) = x^2$?
11. Is $f(x) = \dfrac{x^2-1}{x-1}$ the same function as $g(x) = x + 1$? Explain.

## Level 4 – Neuro
12. **Tuning curve:** $r(\theta) = 10 + 40\, e^{-(\theta - 90°)^2/(2 \cdot 20^2)}$ (in Hz).
    a) Preferred orientation and maximum rate? b) Rate far from the preferred orientation? c) About which axis is the curve symmetric? d) Express $r(110°)$ exactly.
13. **Naka–Rushton:** $R(c) = R_\text{max}\dfrac{c^2}{c^2 + c_{50}^2}$, $c \ge 0$.
    a) $R(0)$? b) $R(c_{50})$? c) Horizontal asymptote? d) Monotonic?
14. **ReLU** $f(x) = \max(0, x)$: zeros, monotonicity, symmetry, range.
