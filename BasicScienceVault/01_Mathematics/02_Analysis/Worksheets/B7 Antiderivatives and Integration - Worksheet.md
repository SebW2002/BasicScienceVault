# B7 Antiderivatives and Integration – Worksheet

> Theory: [[B7 Antiderivatives and Integration]] · Solutions: [[B7 Antiderivatives and Integration - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Analysis]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮.

## Level 1 – Basic antiderivatives
1. $\int x^3\,dx$
2. $\int (6x^2 - 4x + 1)\,dx$
3. $\int \frac1x\,dx$ and $\int e^{3x}\,dx$
4. $\int \left(\sqrt x + \frac{2}{x^2}\right)dx$
5. $\int (\cos x - 2\sin x)\,dx$

## Level 2 – Definite integrals
6. $\int_0^2 x^2\,dx$
7. $\int_1^e \frac1x\,dx$
8. $\int_0^\pi \sin x\,dx$
9. $\int_{-1}^{1} x^3\,dx$ – explain the result geometrically.
10. Compute $\frac{d}{dx}\int_0^x e^{t^2}dt$ (without integrating!).

## Level 3 – Techniques
11. $\int 2x\,e^{x^2}dx$ (substitution)
12. $\int_0^1 x(x^2 + 1)^3dx$ (substitution, transform the bounds)
13. $\int xe^x\,dx$ (by parts)
14. $\int \ln x\,dx$ (by parts with $v' = 1$)
15. Area between $f(x) = x$ and $g(x) = x^2$ on $[0, 1]$.
16. Mean value of $f(x) = x^2$ on $[0, 3]$.

## Level 4 – Neuro
17. **Synaptic charge:** $I(t) = I_0 e^{-t/\tau}$ with $I_0 = 100\,\text{pA}$, $\tau = 5\,\text{ms}$. Compute $Q = \int_0^\infty I(t)\,dt$ (in pC).
18. **Exponential ISI:** show that $p(t) = \lambda e^{-\lambda t}$ integrates to 1 on $[0,\infty)$, and that its mean $\int_0^\infty t\,\lambda e^{-\lambda t}\,dt$ is $\frac1\lambda$.
19. 🧮 **Spike count:** a neuron's rate decays as $r(t) = 50e^{-t/5}$ Hz ($t$ in s). Expected number of spikes in the first 10 s?
