# B2 Exponential Functions – Worksheet

> Theory: [[B2 Exponential Functions]] · Solutions: [[B2 Exponential Functions - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Analysis]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮.

## Level 1 – Basics
1. Evaluate: $e^0$, $\;e^{\ln 5}$, $\;(e^2)^3$, $\;e^3 \cdot e^{-1}$
2. Simplify $\dfrac{e^{2x} \cdot e^{-x}}{e^{x+1}}$.
3. Write $2^x$ and $10^{-x}$ with base $e$.
4. Is $f(x) = 3e^{-x}$ increasing or decreasing? What are $\lim_{x\to\infty} f(x)$ and $f(0)$?

## Level 2 – Equations
5. Solve $e^{3x - 1} = e^2$.
6. Solve $4e^{2t} - 5 = 15$.
7. Solve $e^{-t/2} = 2e^{-t}$.

## Level 3 – Growth & decay
8. Express the fraction remaining of $N(t) = N_0 e^{-t/\tau}$ at $t = \tau, 2\tau, 3\tau$ as powers of $e$.
9. Show that the half-life is $t_{1/2} = \tau\ln 2$.
10. A cell culture doubles every 3 hours. Write $N(t)$ with base 2. How many times larger after 12 hours?

## Level 4 – Neuro
11. **Membrane:** $V_0 = -70\,\text{mV}$, $V_\infty = -50\,\text{mV}$, $\tau = 10\,\text{ms}$.
    a) Write down $V(t)$. b) Check $V(0)$ and $\lim_{t\to\infty}V(t)$. c) Exact value of $V(10\,\text{ms})$.
12. **STDP:** $\Delta w(\Delta t) = 0.01\, e^{-\Delta t/20\,\text{ms}}$. Compute $\Delta w(0)$, $\Delta w(20)$ and the ratio $\Delta w(40)/\Delta w(20)$.
13. **Poisson ISI:** for a neuron firing at $\lambda = 10\,\text{Hz}$, the probability of no spike for time $t$ is $e^{-\lambda t}$. Probability of silence for 100 ms? For 300 ms?
14. 🧮 Compute $V(5\,\text{ms})$ for task 11.
