# B3 Logarithmic Functions – Worksheet

> Theory: [[B3 Logarithmic Functions]] · Solutions: [[B3 Logarithmic Functions - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Analysis]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮.

## Level 1 – Domain, zeros, inverse
1. Domain of a) $\ln(2x - 4)$ b) $\ln(x^2)$ c) $\ln(x^2 + 1)$
2. Zero of $f(x) = \ln(x - 3)$.
3. Inverse function of $f(x) = \ln(x + 2)$.
4. For which $x$ is $\ln x < 0$?
5. Describe how $y = \ln(x - 1) + 2$ arises from $y = \ln x$. New vertical asymptote?

## Level 2 – Equations
6. Solve $\ln x = 2$ and $\ln(3x) = 0$.
7. Simplify $\ln(e^{3x}) - \ln(e^{x})$.
8. Solve $\ln x + \ln(x - 2) = \ln 3$. Check the domain!

## Level 3 – Linearising
9. Linearise $y = 5e^{-0.2t}$ by taking $\ln$. What are the slope and intercept? What is $\tau$?
10. Linearise $y = 3x^2$ with a log–log transform. Slope?
11. A log–log plot shows a straight line with slope $-1$ through the point $(x, y) = (1, 10)$. Which function is this?
12. Which grows faster for large $x$: $\ln x$ or $x^{0.1}$?

## Level 4 – Neuro
13. **Weber–Fechner** $S = k\ln(I/I_0)$:
    a) By how much does $S$ increase if $I$ doubles? Does it depend on the starting intensity?
    b) By which factor must $I$ grow to increase $S$ by $2k\ln 2$?
14. **Entropy:** a neuron responds with three possible outputs with probabilities $\tfrac12, \tfrac14, \tfrac14$. Compute $H = -\sum p_i\log_2 p_i$.
15. 🧮 **Time constant:** a PSP decays from $10\,\text{mV}$ at $t = 0$ to $4\,\text{mV}$ at $t = 4\,\text{ms}$ (single exponential). Compute $\tau$.
