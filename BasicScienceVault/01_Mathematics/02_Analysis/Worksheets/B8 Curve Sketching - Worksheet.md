# B8 Curve Sketching – Worksheet

> Theory: [[B8 Curve Sketching]] · Solutions: [[B8 Curve Sketching - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Analysis]]
> No calculator needed. "Full sketch" = symmetry, zeros, extrema, inflection points, behaviour at the boundaries.

## Level 1 – Polynomials
1. $f(x) = x^2 - 4x + 3$: zeros and extremum.
2. $f(x) = x^3 - 3x$: full sketch (compare with the worked example in the theory note – try without looking!).
3. $f(x) = x^3 - 6x^2 + 9x$: full sketch.

## Level 2 – Higher degree & rational
4. $f(x) = x^4 - 2x^2$: full sketch.
5. $f(x) = \dfrac{x}{x^2 + 1}$: symmetry, zero, asymptote, extrema.
6. Does $f(x) = x^3$ have an extremum at $x = 0$? Explain.

## Level 3 – Exponential & log
7. $f(x) = xe^{-x}$: zero, behaviour for $x\to\pm\infty$, extremum, inflection point.
8. $f(x) = \dfrac{\ln x}{x}$, $x > 0$: zero and maximum.

## Level 4 – Neuro & optimisation
9. **Alpha function** $g(t) = t\,e^{-t/\tau}$, $t \ge 0$: peak time, peak value, inflection point.
10. **Sigmoid** $\sigma(x) = \dfrac1{1 + e^{-x}}$: show it is monotonically increasing, give both horizontal asymptotes, and show that the inflection point is at $x = 0$. (Hint: $\sigma' = \sigma(1-\sigma)$, so $\sigma'' = \sigma'(1 - 2\sigma)$.)
11. **Gaussian tuning** $f(x) = e^{-x^2/2}$: maximum and inflection points. Where is the curve steepest?
12. **Optimisation:** a rectangle has perimeter 20. Which side lengths maximise the area?
