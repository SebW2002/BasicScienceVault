# B4 Trigonometric Functions – Worksheet

> Theory: [[B4 Trigonometric Functions]] · Solutions: [[B4 Trigonometric Functions - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Analysis]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮. Special values: see [[D4 Unit Circle Trigonometry]].

## Level 1 – Values & conversion
1. Evaluate: $\sin\frac\pi6$, $\;\cos\frac\pi3$, $\;\tan\frac\pi4$, $\;\sin\frac{3\pi}2$, $\;\cos\pi$
2. Convert to radians: $45°$, $120°$, $270°$. Convert $\frac{5\pi}6$ to degrees.
3. Amplitude and period of: a) $3\sin(2x)$ b) $-2\cos(x/2)$ c) $\sin(2\pi f t)$ with $f = 10\,\text{Hz}$

## Level 2 – Identities
4. Simplify $\sin^2 x + \cos^2 x + \tan x\cos x$.
5. Simplify $\sin(x + \pi)$ and $\cos(\frac\pi2 - x)$.
6. Is $\sin^2 x$ even or odd? Is $\sin x\cos x$?
7. Use the double-angle formula to compute $\sin\frac\pi{12}\cos\frac\pi{12}$.
8. Use an addition theorem to compute $\sin 75°$ exactly.

## Level 3 – Equations
9. Solve $\sin x = \frac12$ on $[0, 2\pi)$.
10. Solve $\cos(2x) = 0$ on $[0, 2\pi)$.
11. Solve $2\cos^2 x - 1 = 0$ on $[0, 2\pi)$.

## Level 4 – Neuro
12. Write the signal of a 10 Hz alpha oscillation with amplitude $5\,\mu\text{V}$, phase 0 and offset 0. What is the period?
13. **Cosine tuning:** $r(\theta) = 20 + 15\cos(\theta - 60°)$ Hz. a) Preferred direction and maximum rate? b) Direction and value of the minimum? c) $r(150°)$?
14. **Phase lag:** two 8 Hz oscillations; the second lags by $\frac1{32}\,\text{s}$. Phase difference in radians?
15. 🧮 Value of a 40 Hz gamma oscillation $\sin(2\pi\cdot 40\cdot t)$ at $t = 3\,\text{ms}$.
