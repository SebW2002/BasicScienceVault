# B5 Limits and Continuity – Worksheet

> Theory: [[B5 Limits and Continuity]] · Solutions: [[B5 Limits and Continuity - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Analysis]]
> No calculator needed.

## Level 1 – Plug in / simplify
1. $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to2}(3x^2 - 1)$
2. $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to3}\frac{x^2 - 9}{x - 3}$
3. $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\frac{x^2 + 2x}{x}$
4. $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to-1}\frac{x^2 + 3x + 2}{x + 1}$

## Level 2 – Infinity
5. $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{3x^2 + 1}{x^2 - 5}$
6. $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{2x + 1}{x^2 + 1}$
7. $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{x^3}{x^2 + 1}$
8. $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0^+}\frac1x$ and $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0^-}\frac1x$. Does $\lim_{x\to0}\frac1x$ exist?
9. $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty} x^2 e^{-x}$

## Level 3 – Tricks & standard limits
10. $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to4}\frac{\sqrt x - 2}{x - 4}$
11. $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin(3x)}{x}$
12. $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1 + \frac2n\right)^n$
13. $\displaystyle\lim_{h\to0}\frac{(2+h)^2 - 4}{h}$ – what does this limit represent?

## Level 4 – Continuity & neuro
14. Find $a$ such that $f(x) = \begin{cases} x + 1 & x < 1 \\ ax & x \ge 1\end{cases}$ is continuous.
15. Classify the discontinuities: a) $\frac{x^2-1}{x-1}$ at $x = 1$ b) $\frac1{x-2}$ at $x = 2$ c) Heaviside $H(x) = \begin{cases}0 & x<0\\1 & x\ge0\end{cases}$ at $x = 0$
16. **Membrane:** $V(t) = 20\left(1 - e^{-t/5}\right)$. Compute $\lim_{t\to\infty}V(t)$ and $\lim_{t\to0}V(t)$.
17. **Firing rate:** a neuron emits 3 spikes in a 50 ms window. Estimate the rate. Why is this only an approximation of the instantaneous rate?
