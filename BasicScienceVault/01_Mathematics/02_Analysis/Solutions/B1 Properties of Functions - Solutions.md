# B1 Properties of Functions – Solutions

> Worksheet: [[B1 Properties of Functions - Worksheet]] · Theory: [[B1 Properties of Functions]] · Wrong? → [[Mistakes - Analysis]]

## Level 1
1. a) $\mathbb R \setminus \{2\}$ b) $x \ge -3$ c) $x > 1$
2. a) $x = \pm3$ b) $x(x-2)(x+2) = 0 \Rightarrow x \in \{0, \pm2\}$ c) $e^x = 1 \Rightarrow x = 0$
3. a) even (only even powers) b) odd c) neither ($f(-x) = x^2 - x$) d) even e) even: $(-x)\sin(-x) = (-x)(-\sin x) = x\sin x$

## Level 2
4. a) $\dfrac{x+1}{(x-2)(x+2)}$: poles at $x = \pm2$, no cancellation.
   b) $\dfrac{x-1}{(x-1)(x+1)} = \dfrac{1}{x+1}$: removable discontinuity at $x = 1$, pole at $x = -1$.
5. Vertical: $x = 3$. Horizontal: equal degrees → $y = \frac21 = 2$.
6. Shift right by 1, stretch vertically by 2, shift up by 3. Vertex $(1, 3)$.
7. $f(g(x)) = (x+1)^2$; $\;g(f(x)) = x^2 + 1$ – different!
8. a) $y = 3x - 6 \Rightarrow x = \frac{y + 6}{3}$ → $f^{-1}(x) = \dfrac{x+6}{3}$
   b) $y = e^{2x} \Rightarrow x = \tfrac12 \ln y$ → $f^{-1}(x) = \tfrac12\ln x$ for $x > 0$

## Level 3
9. $f(-x) = \dfrac{-x}{1 + x^2} = -f(x)$ ✓ odd. Zero: $x = 0$. Degree numerator < degree denominator → asymptote $y = 0$.
10. $-2x + 3$: decreasing everywhere (slope $-2$). $x^2$: decreasing on $(-\infty, 0]$, increasing on $[0, \infty)$.
11. Not quite: $f$ is undefined at $x = 1$, $g$ is defined there ($g(1) = 2$). Everywhere else they agree – $f$ has a removable discontinuity (hole) at $(1, 2)$.

## Level 4
12. a) $\theta_\text{pref} = 90°$, $r_\text{max} = 10 + 40 = 50\,\text{Hz}$ b) exponential → 0, so $r \to 10\,\text{Hz}$ (baseline) c) symmetric about $\theta = 90°$ d) $r(110°) = 10 + 40e^{-400/800} = 10 + 40e^{-1/2}$ ($\approx 34\,\text{Hz}$)
13. a) 0 b) $R_\text{max}\frac{c_{50}^2}{2c_{50}^2} = \frac{R_\text{max}}{2}$ (that's why it's called $c_{50}$) c) $y = R_\text{max}$ d) yes, increasing for $c \ge 0$ (more contrast → larger numerator share)
14. Zeros: all $x \le 0$. Monotonically non-decreasing (constant, then increasing). Neither even nor odd. Range $[0, \infty)$.
