# B2 Exponential Functions – Solutions

> Worksheet: [[B2 Exponential Functions - Worksheet]] · Theory: [[B2 Exponential Functions]] · Wrong? → [[Mistakes - Analysis]]

## Level 1
1. $1$; $\;5$; $\;e^6$; $\;e^2$
2. $e^{2x - x - (x+1)} = e^{-1} = \dfrac1e$ (independent of $x$)
3. $2^x = e^{x\ln2}$; $\;10^{-x} = e^{-x\ln10}$
4. Decreasing (base $e^{-1} < 1$, or: $f' = -3e^{-x} < 0$). Limit 0, $f(0) = 3$.

## Level 2
5. $3x - 1 = 2 \Rightarrow x = 1$
6. $4e^{2t} = 20 \Rightarrow e^{2t} = 5 \Rightarrow t = \tfrac12 \ln 5$
7. Multiply by $e^{t}$: $e^{t/2} = 2 \Rightarrow t/2 = \ln2 \Rightarrow t = 2\ln2$

## Level 3
8. $e^{-1}$ (≈ 37 %), $e^{-2}$ (≈ 14 %), $e^{-3}$ (≈ 5 %)
9. $e^{-t_{1/2}/\tau} = \tfrac12 \Rightarrow -t_{1/2}/\tau = \ln\tfrac12 = -\ln2 \Rightarrow t_{1/2} = \tau\ln2$
10. $N(t) = N_0\, 2^{t/3}$; $N(12) = N_0 2^4 = 16N_0$

## Level 4
11. a) $V(t) = -50 + (-70 - (-50))e^{-t/10} = -50 - 20e^{-t/10}$
    b) $V(0) = -50 - 20 = -70$ ✓; $e^{-t/10} \to 0$ → $V \to -50$ ✓
    c) $V(10) = -50 - 20e^{-1} = -50 - \frac{20}{e}$ ($\approx -57.4\,\text{mV}$)
12. $\Delta w(0) = 0.01$; $\;\Delta w(20) = 0.01e^{-1}$; ratio $= \dfrac{e^{-2}}{e^{-1}} = e^{-1}$ – every additional 20 ms multiplies by $1/e$.
13. $\lambda t = 10 \cdot 0.1 = 1 \Rightarrow e^{-1} \approx 0.37$; $\;\lambda t = 3 \Rightarrow e^{-3} \approx 0.05$
14. $V(5) = -50 - 20e^{-0.5} \approx -50 - 20 \cdot 0.6065 \approx -62.1\,\text{mV}$
