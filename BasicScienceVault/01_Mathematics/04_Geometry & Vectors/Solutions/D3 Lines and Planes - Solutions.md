# D3 Lines and Planes – Solutions

## Level 1
1. $m = \frac{7 - 3}{3 - 1} = 2$; $3 = 2\cdot1 + b\Rightarrow b = 1$ → $y = 2x + 1$
2. $\mathbf x = (0, 1, 2) + t(2, 4, 2)$ (or direction $(1, 2, 1)$)
3. $(5,0,5)$: $1 + 2t = 5\Rightarrow t = 2$; check $2 - 2 = 0$ ✓, $3 + 2 = 5$ ✓ → on $g$.
   $(3,1,5)$: $t = 1$ from $x$; $y$: $2 - 1 = 1$ ✓; $z$: $3 + 1 = 4\neq5$ ✗ → not on $g$.

## Level 2
4. $x + y + z = 1$ (all three points satisfy it)
5. $2x - y + 3z = 2 - 1 + 3 = 4$
6. $\mathbf n = (1,1,0)\times(0,1,1) = (1\cdot1 - 0\cdot1,\ 0\cdot0 - 1\cdot1,\ 1\cdot1 - 1\cdot0) = (1, -1, 1)$; $d = \mathbf n\cdot(1,0,0) = 1$ → $x - y + z = 1$
7. Same normal, $d = 0$: $2x + y - z = 0$

## Level 3
8. $(1 + t) + t + t = 7\Rightarrow t = 2$ → $(3, 2, 2)$
9. $\frac{\lvert 4 - 1 + 6 - 3\rvert}{\sqrt{4 + 1 + 4}} = \frac63 = 2$
10. Directions $(1,2,3)$ and $(2,4,6)$ are multiples → parallel. Is $(1,0,0)$ on the second line? $s(2,4,6) = (1,0,0)$ would need $s = \tfrac12$ and $s = 0$ → no. **Parallel, not identical.**
11. Normals $(1,1,0)$, $(0,1,1)$: $\cos\theta = \frac{1}{\sqrt2\sqrt2} = \frac12\Rightarrow\theta = 60°$

## Level 4
12. a) $\mathbf T - \mathbf E = (-12, -18, -36)$ → $\mathbf x(t) = (24, 8, 40) + t(-12, -18, -36)$, $t\in[0, 1]$
    b) $\lVert(-12, -18, -36)\rVert = 6\lVert(-2, -3, -6)\rVert = 6\sqrt{4 + 9 + 36} = 6\cdot7 = 42\,\text{mm}$
    c) $t = \tfrac34$: $(24 - 9,\ 8 - 13.5,\ 40 - 27) = (15, -5.5, 13)$
13. a) $2r_1 - r_2 - 3 = 0$, i.e. $r_2 = 2r_1 - 3$ (a line)
    b) $\mathbf r_1$: $6 - 1 - 3 = 2 > 0$ → A; $\mathbf r_2$: $-3 < 0$ → B
    c) $\frac{2}{\lVert\mathbf w\rVert} = \frac{2}{\sqrt5}$
14. Coronal: the plane $y = 12$ (normal $(0, 1, 0)$). Intersection of $z = 10$ and $x = -20$: $\mathbf x = (-20, 0, 10) + t(0, 1, 0)$ – a line running anterior–posterior.
