# D2 Dot and Cross Product – Solutions

> Worksheet: [[D2 Dot and Cross Product - Worksheet]] · Theory: [[D2 Dot and Cross Product]] · Wrong? → [[Mistakes - Geometry and Vectors]]

## Level 1
1. $4 - 10 + 18 = 12$
2. $\cos\theta = \frac{1}{1\cdot\sqrt2} = \frac{\sqrt2}2\Rightarrow\theta = 45°$
3. $2 - 4 + 2 = 0\Rightarrow\theta = 90°$
4. $6 - k + 4 = 0\Rightarrow k = 10$
5. Onto $\mathbf b$: $\frac{3}{1}(1, 0) = (3, 0)$. Onto $\mathbf c$: $\frac{3 + 4}{2}(1, 1) = \left(\tfrac72, \tfrac72\right)$

## Level 2
6. $(0, 0, 1)$ and $(0, 0, -1)$ (anticommutative)
7. $(2\cdot6 - 3\cdot5,\ 3\cdot4 - 1\cdot6,\ 1\cdot5 - 2\cdot4) = (-3, 6, -3)$.
   $(-3,6,-3)\cdot(1,2,3) = -3 + 12 - 9 = 0$ ✓; $\;(-3,6,-3)\cdot(4,5,6) = -12 + 30 - 18 = 0$ ✓
8. $\vec{AB} = (-1, 1, 0)$, $\vec{AC} = (-1, 0, 1)$, $\vec{AB}\times\vec{AC} = (1\cdot1 - 0\cdot0,\ 0\cdot(-1) - (-1)\cdot1,\ (-1)\cdot0 - 1\cdot(-1)) = (1, 1, 1)$.
   Area $= \tfrac12\sqrt3$.
9. $\mathbf b\times\mathbf c = (2\cdot3 - 0\cdot1,\ 0\cdot0 - 1\cdot3,\ 1\cdot1 - 2\cdot0) = (6, -3, 1)$; $\;\mathbf a\cdot(6, -3, 1) = 6$ → volume 6.
10. $\mathbf 0$ – a vector is parallel to itself ($\sin0 = 0$).

## Level 3
11. $u = 2 - 1 + 2 = 3$. Maximum for $\mathbf x$ parallel to $\mathbf w$: $\mathbf x = \frac{\mathbf w}{\lVert\mathbf w\rVert} = \frac{(1, -1, 0.5)}{1.5} = \left(\tfrac23, -\tfrac23, \tfrac13\right)$ (since $\lVert\mathbf w\rVert = \sqrt{2.25} = 1.5$).
12. $\frac{2 + 2 + 4}{3\cdot3} = \frac89\approx0.89$. Doubling $\mathbf r_B$ doesn't change it – cosine similarity ignores overall gain.
13. Normal $(1, 1, 1)$ (task 8) → unit normal $\frac1{\sqrt3}(1, 1, 1)$.
14. a) $(0,0,2)\times(0,0,5) = \mathbf 0$ – parallel → no field from a radial dipole.
    b) $(1,0,0)\times(0,0,5) = (0\cdot5 - 0\cdot0,\ 0\cdot0 - 1\cdot5,\ 1\cdot0 - 0\cdot0) = (0, -5, 0)$ – non-zero.
    MEG mainly sees tangential sources (sulcal walls), not radial ones (gyral crowns).
15. $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b = 32$, $\lVert\mathbf a\rVert = \sqrt{14}$, $\lVert\mathbf b\rVert = \sqrt{77}$ → $\cos\theta = \frac{32}{\sqrt{1078}}\approx0.9746\Rightarrow\theta\approx12.9°$
