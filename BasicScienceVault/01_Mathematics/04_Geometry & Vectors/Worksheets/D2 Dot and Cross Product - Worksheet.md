# D2 Dot and Cross Product – Worksheet

> Theory: [[D2 Dot and Cross Product]] · Solutions: [[D2 Dot and Cross Product - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Geometry and Vectors]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮.

## Level 1 – Dot product
1. $(1, 2, 3)\cdot(4, -5, 6)$
2. Angle between $(1, 0)$ and $(1, 1)$.
3. Angle between $(1, 2, 2)$ and $(2, -2, 1)$.
4. Find $k$ such that $(2, k, 1)\perp(3, -1, 4)$.
5. Projection of $\mathbf a = (3, 4)$ onto $\mathbf b = (1, 0)$ and onto $\mathbf c = (1, 1)$.

## Level 2 – Cross product
6. $(1, 0, 0)\times(0, 1, 0)$ and $(0, 1, 0)\times(1, 0, 0)$.
7. $(1, 2, 3)\times(4, 5, 6)$. Check that the result is perpendicular to both vectors.
8. Area of the triangle with vertices $A = (1, 0, 0)$, $B = (0, 1, 0)$, $C = (0, 0, 1)$.
9. Volume of the parallelepiped spanned by $\mathbf a = (1, 0, 0)$, $\mathbf b = (1, 2, 0)$, $\mathbf c = (0, 1, 3)$ (triple product).
10. What is $\mathbf a\times\mathbf a$? Why?

## Level 3 – Neuro
11. **Linear neuron / receptive field:** weights $\mathbf w = (1, -1, 0.5)$, stimulus $\mathbf x = (2, 1, 4)$. Response $u = \mathbf w\cdot\mathbf x$? Which stimulus of length $\lVert\mathbf x\rVert = 1$ maximises the response?
12. **Cosine similarity:** population responses $\mathbf r_A = (2, 1, 2)$ and $\mathbf r_B = (1, 2, 2)$. Compute the cosine similarity. What happens if all rates in $\mathbf r_B$ are doubled?
13. **Cortical normal:** a mesh triangle has the vertices $A$, $B$, $C$ from task 8. Give the unit normal vector.
14. **MEG:** compute $\mathbf Q\times\mathbf r$ for a dipole at the origin and sensor direction $\mathbf r = (0, 0, 5)$: a) radial dipole $\mathbf Q = (0, 0, 2)$ b) tangential dipole $\mathbf Q = (1, 0, 0)$. What does this say about MEG sensitivity?
15. 🧮 Angle between $(1, 2, 3)$ and $(4, 5, 6)$ in degrees.
