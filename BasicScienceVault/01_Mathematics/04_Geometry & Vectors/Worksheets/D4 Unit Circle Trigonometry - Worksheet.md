# D4 Unit Circle Trigonometry – Worksheet

> Theory: [[D4 Unit Circle Trigonometry]] · Solutions: [[D4 Unit Circle Trigonometry - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Geometry and Vectors]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮.

## Level 1 – Special values
1. Fill in from memory: $\sin$ and $\cos$ at $0, \frac\pi6, \frac\pi4, \frac\pi3, \frac\pi2$.
2. Compute $\sin150°$, $\cos210°$, $\tan300°$.
3. Compute $\sin\left(-\frac\pi4\right)$, $\cos\frac{5\pi}3$, $\sin\frac{7\pi}6$.
4. In which quadrant is $\theta$ if $\sin\theta < 0$ and $\cos\theta > 0$?

## Level 2 – Relations & triangles
5. $\cos\theta = \frac35$ and $\theta$ is in quadrant IV. Find $\sin\theta$ and $\tan\theta$.
6. Right triangle with hypotenuse 10 and an angle of $30°$: lengths of both legs.
7. Show that $1 + \tan^2\theta = \frac{1}{\cos^2\theta}$.
8. Law of cosines: $a = 3$, $b = 5$, $\gamma = 60°$. Find $c$.

## Level 3 – Polar coordinates
9. Convert $(r, \theta) = (2, \frac\pi3)$ to Cartesian coordinates.
10. Convert $(-1, 1)$ to polar coordinates.
11. Convert $(0, -3)$ and $(-2, 0)$ to polar coordinates.

## Level 4 – Neuro
12. **Phase locking:** a neuron fires three spikes at LFP phases $0$, $\frac\pi2$ and $\pi$. Compute the mean resultant vector, its length $R$ and the mean phase.
13. **Phase locking:** two spikes at phases $\frac\pi6$ and $-\frac\pi6$. Mean phase and $R$?
14. **Why not average numbers?** Two spikes at $350°$ and $10°$. What is the arithmetic mean? What is the circular mean?
15. **Head direction:** preferred direction $120°$, tuning $r = 20 + 15\cos(\theta - 120°)$. Rate when the animal faces $300°$? And $180°$?
16. 🧮 Polar angle (in degrees, $0$–$360°$) of the point $(-4, -3)$.
