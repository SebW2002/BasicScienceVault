# D1 Vectors in 2D and 3D – Worksheet

> Theory: [[D1 Vectors in 2D and 3D]] · Solutions: [[D1 Vectors in 2D and 3D - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Geometry and Vectors]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮. Let $\mathbf a = (2, -1, 3)^T$, $\mathbf b = (1, 4, -2)^T$.

## Level 1 – Arithmetic
1. Compute $\mathbf a + \mathbf b$ and $2\mathbf a - 3\mathbf b$.
2. Length of $(3, 4)$ and of $(1, 2, 2)$.
3. Unit vector in the direction of $(3, 0, 4)$.
4. For $P = (1, 2, 3)$ and $Q = (4, 6, 3)$: compute $\vec{PQ}$, the distance $d(P, Q)$ and the midpoint.

## Level 2 – Linear combinations
5. Write $(5, 7)$ as a linear combination of $(1, 1)$ and $(1, 2)$.
6. Are $(1, 2, 3)$ and $(2, 4, 6)$ linearly independent?
7. Are $(1, 0, 1)$, $(0, 1, 1)$, $(1, 1, 0)$ linearly independent?
8. Find all $x$ with $\lVert(x, 3)\rVert = 5$.
9. Find the point that divides the segment from $A = (0, 0, 0)$ to $B = (3, 6, 9)$ in the ratio $1:2$.

## Level 3 – Neuro
10. **Electrodes:** in MNI space (mm), $E_1 = (-40, 20, 30)$ and $E_2 = (-40, 50, 70)$. Distance? In which anatomical direction(s) does $E_2$ lie relative to $E_1$?
11. **Population vector:** three neurons have preferred directions $\mathbf c_1 = (1, 0)$, $\mathbf c_2 = (0, 1)$, $\mathbf c_3 = (-1, 0)$ and fire at $10$, $20$, $4$ Hz. Compute $\mathbf P = \sum r_i\mathbf c_i$. Roughly which direction is decoded?
12. **State space:** two neurons respond to stimulus A with $(10, 2)$ Hz and to B with $(4, 10)$ Hz. Distance between the two population responses? Which response is closer to a test response $(8, 4)$?
13. 🧮 **Source localisation:** distance between the estimated source $(12.5, -33.0, 41.2)$ and the true source $(-8.3, 10.4, 25.9)$ (mm).
