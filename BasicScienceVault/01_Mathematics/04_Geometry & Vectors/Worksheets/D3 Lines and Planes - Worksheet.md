# D3 Lines and Planes – Worksheet

> Theory: [[D3 Lines and Planes]] · Solutions: [[D3 Lines and Planes - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Geometry and Vectors]]
> No calculator needed.

## Level 1 – Lines
1. 2D: line through $(1, 3)$ and $(3, 7)$ in the form $y = mx + b$.
2. Parametric form of the line through $P = (0, 1, 2)$ and $Q = (2, 5, 4)$.
3. Line $g: \mathbf x = (1, 2, 3) + t(2, -1, 1)$. Is $(5, 0, 5)$ on $g$? Is $(3, 1, 5)$?

## Level 2 – Planes
4. Coordinate form of the plane through $(1,0,0)$, $(0,1,0)$, $(0,0,1)$.
5. Plane with normal vector $(2, -1, 3)$ through $(1, 1, 1)$.
6. Convert $E: \mathbf x = (1, 0, 0) + s(1, 1, 0) + t(0, 1, 1)$ into coordinate form.
7. Plane parallel to $2x + y - z = 5$ through the origin.

## Level 3 – Intersections, distances, angles
8. Intersection point of $\mathbf x = (1, 0, 0) + t(1, 1, 1)$ with the plane $x + y + z = 7$.
9. Distance of the point $(2, 1, 3)$ from the plane $2x - y + 2z = 3$.
10. Are the lines $\mathbf x = (1, 0, 0) + t(1, 2, 3)$ and $\mathbf x = s(2, 4, 6)$ parallel, identical or neither?
11. Angle between the planes $x + y = 1$ and $y + z = 2$.

## Level 4 – Neuro
12. **DBS trajectory:** entry point $E = (24, 8, 40)$, target $T = (12, -10, 4)$ (mm).
    a) Parametric form of the trajectory. b) Length of the path. c) The point 3/4 of the way to the target.
13. **Linear decoder:** $\mathbf w = (2, -1)$, $b = -3$ (2 neurons).
    a) Equation of the decision boundary. b) Classify $\mathbf r_1 = (3, 1)$ and $\mathbf r_2 = (0, 0)$ (class A if $\mathbf w\cdot\mathbf r + b > 0$). c) Distance of $\mathbf r_1$ to the boundary.
14. **MRI:** which plane is a coronal slice at $y = 12$? Give the intersection of the axial slice $z = 10$ with the sagittal slice $x = -20$ as a parametric line.
