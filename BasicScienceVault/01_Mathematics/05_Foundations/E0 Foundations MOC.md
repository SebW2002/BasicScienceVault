# E0 Foundations MOC – Language & Cross-cutting Topics

> [[00 Roadmap|↑ Roadmap]] · ⚠️ [[Mistakes - Foundations]]

These topics don't sit at one point in the learning path – they are the **language** (sets, logic) and **complex numbers**, which turned out to be essential for the neuroscience part (Fourier analysis of EEG/MEG, MRI k-space, oscillating dynamics).

| # | Topic | What it's about | When | Practice |

| E1 | [[E1 Sets Logic and Quantifiers]] | notation, implications, $\forall/\exists$, necessary vs. sufficient | skim at the start, return whenever notation confuses you | [[E1 Sets Logic and Quantifiers - Worksheet\|✏️]] · [[E1 Sets Logic and Quantifiers - Solutions\|✅]] |
| E2 | [[E2 Complex Numbers]] | $i$, polar form, Euler's formula, Fourier & phase | after [[D4 Unit Circle Trigonometry]] and [[C4 Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors]], before [[B12 Fourier Analysis]] | [[E2 Complex Numbers - Worksheet\|✏️]] · [[E2 Complex Numbers - Solutions\|✅]] |

## Where they show up
- Set notation for domains and solution sets → [[B1 Properties of Functions]], [[A8 Solving Equations]]
- Quantifiers in definitions of limits and continuity → [[B5 Limits and Continuity]]
- Complex roots of quadratics → [[A8 Solving Equations]]
- Euler's formula ← [[B9 Taylor Series]] + [[B4 Trigonometric Functions]] + [[B2 Exponential Functions]]
- Complex eigenvalues = oscillations → [[C4 Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors]]
- Polar form = unit circle → [[D4 Unit Circle Trigonometry]]
- Complex form of Fourier series and transforms → [[B12 Fourier Analysis]]
- In the neuroscience part: [[Ph09 Waves and Oscillations]], [[CS11 Time Series and Signal Processing]], [[Meth03 Electrophysiology]], [[Meth04 In Vivo Imaging]] (k-space)
