# E1 Sets, Logic and Quantifiers – Worksheet

> Theory: [[E1 Sets Logic and Quantifiers]] · Solutions: [[E1 Sets Logic and Quantifiers - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Foundations]]
> No calculator needed.

## Level 1 – Sets
1. $A = \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$, $B = \{3, 4, 5\}$. Compute $A\cup B$, $A\cap B$, $A\setminus B$, $B\setminus A$.
2. Write as an interval: $\{x\in\mathbb R\mid -2\le x < 5\}$ and $\{x\in\mathbb R\mid x^2\le9\}$.
3. True or false? a) $-3\in\mathbb N$ b) $\sqrt2\in\mathbb Q$ c) $0.5\in\mathbb Q$ d) $\mathbb N\subseteq\mathbb Z$ e) $\emptyset = \{0\}$
4. Compute $A\times B$ for $A = \{a, b\}$, $B = \{1, 2\}$.
5. List the power set of $\{1, 2\}$. How many subsets does a set with 5 elements have?

## Level 2 – Logic
6. Verify De Morgan's law $(A\cup B)^c = A^c\cap B^c$ with universal set $\{1,\dots,6\}$, $A = \{1,2,3\}$, $B = \{3,4\}$.
7. Write down the truth table of $p\Rightarrow q$ and of $\neg q\Rightarrow\neg p$. What do you notice?
8. Is $(p\wedge q)\Rightarrow p$ always true (a tautology)? Is $p\Rightarrow(p\wedge q)$?
9. Fill in "necessary" or "sufficient": a) $x > 5$ is ___ for $x > 2$. b) $x > 2$ is ___ for $x > 5$.

## Level 3 – Quantifiers
10. Negate: "$\forall x\in\mathbb R: x^2\ge0$". Is the negation true?
11. Negate: "$\exists x\in\mathbb R: x^2 = -1$".
12. Which is true in $\mathbb R$? a) $\forall x\ \exists y: y = x + 1$ b) $\exists y\ \forall x: y = x + 1$
13. Translate into words: $\forall\varepsilon > 0\ \exists N\in\mathbb N\ \forall n\ge N: \lvert a_n - L\rvert < \varepsilon$. What concept is this?

## Level 4 – Neuro reasoning
14. **Inclusion–exclusion:** of 120 recorded neurons, 50 respond to faces, 40 to houses and 15 to both. How many respond to at least one category? To neither?
15. **Negation:** negate "there is a neuron that fires for every stimulus".
16. **Contrapositive vs. converse:** statement S: "If the hippocampus is lesioned, no new episodic memories are formed." Write the contrapositive and the converse. Which one is logically equivalent to S?
17. **Causality:** an inactivation of area X abolishes behaviour Y; optogenetic stimulation of X does not evoke Y. Is X necessary, sufficient, both or neither for Y (according to these experiments)?
