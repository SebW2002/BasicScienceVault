# E2 Complex Numbers – Worksheet

> Theory: [[E2 Complex Numbers]] · Solutions: [[E2 Complex Numbers - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Foundations]]
> No calculator needed.

## Level 1 – Arithmetic
1. $(3 + 2i) + (1 - 5i)$
2. $(2 + i)(3 - 2i)$
3. $i^3$, $i^4$, $i^{10}$
4. Conjugate and modulus of $3 - 4i$.
5. $\dfrac{1 + 2i}{1 - i}$

## Level 2 – Equations & properties
6. Solve $z^2 + 4 = 0$ and $z^2 - 2z + 5 = 0$.
7. Show that $z\bar z = \lvert z\rvert^2$ for $z = a + bi$.

## Level 3 – Polar form
8. Polar form of $1 + i$, $-2$, and $-3i$.
9. Cartesian form of $2e^{i\pi/3}$ and of $e^{i\pi/2}$.
10. Compute $(1 + i)^4$ using the polar form.
11. Verify $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$ using Euler's formula.

## Level 4 – Neuro
12. **Fourier coefficient:** at 10 Hz the Fourier transform of an EEG signal is $X = 3 - 3i$. Amplitude, power and phase?
13. **PLV:** in two trials the phase at stimulus onset is $0$ and $\frac\pi2$. Compute $\text{PLV} = \left\lvert\frac12(e^{i0} + e^{i\pi/2})\right\rvert$.
14. **Dynamics:** a linearised E–I circuit has eigenvalues $\lambda = -1\pm2i$ (time in units of 10 ms). Does activity grow or decay? What is the angular frequency and period of the oscillation?
