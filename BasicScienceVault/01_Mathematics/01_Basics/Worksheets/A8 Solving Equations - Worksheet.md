## Level 1 – Linear
1. $3x - 7 = 11$
2. $5(x - 2) = 2x + 8$

## Level 2 – Quadratic & product
5. $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$
6. $2x^2 - 8x = 0$

8. $x^2 + 2x + 5 = 0$
9. $x^2 = 2x$ (don't divide by $x$!)

## Level 3 – Other types

## Level 4 – Formulas & neuro
16. Rearrange: a) $\tau = RC$ for $C$; b) $I = g(V - E)$ for $V$; c) $E = \dfrac{RT}{zF}\ln\dfrac{c_o}{c_i}$ for $c_i$.
17. **Membrane charging:** $V(t) = 20\left(1 - e^{-t/10}\right)\,\text{mV}$ ($t$ in ms). When does $V$ reach $10\,\text{mV}$? (exact form) Does it ever reach $25\,\text{mV}$?
18. **Steady state:** $\tau \dfrac{dV}{dt} = -(V + 70) + 15$. Find $V$ where $\dfrac{dV}{dt} = 0$.
19. 🧮 **Time to threshold:** $V_\infty = 30\,\text{mV}$, $\tau = 20\,\text{ms}$, threshold at $V_\theta = 25\,\text{mV}$ above rest. Compute $t = -\tau\ln(1 - V_\theta/V_\infty)$.
