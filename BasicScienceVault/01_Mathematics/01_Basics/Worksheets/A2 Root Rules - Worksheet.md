# A2 Root Rules – Worksheet

> Theory: [[A2 Root Rules]] · Solutions: [[A2 Root Rules - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Basics]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮.

## Level 1 – Warm-up
1. Simplify $\sqrt{50}$.
2. Simplify $\sqrt{12} + \sqrt{27}$.
3. Compute $\sqrt{8} \cdot \sqrt{2}$.
4. Simplify $\sqrt[3]{54}$.
5. Write as a power: $\sqrt[4]{x^3}$ and $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x}}$.

## Level 2 – Standard
6. Simplify $\sqrt{x^6}$ for arbitrary real $x$.
7. Rationalise the denominator: $\dfrac{6}{\sqrt3}$.
8. Rationalise the denominator: $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt5 - 2}$.
9. Compute $(\sqrt7 + \sqrt3)(\sqrt7 - \sqrt3)$.
10. Simplify $\sqrt{x\sqrt{x}}$ (for $x \ge 0$) and write it as a single power.
11. Simplify $\sqrt{18 a^3 b^2}$ for $a, b \ge 0$.

## Level 3 – Equations & applications
12. Solve $\sqrt{x+3} = 5$.
13. Solve $\sqrt{2x+1} = x - 1$. **Check your solutions!**
14. **Neuro (SEM):** A neuron's firing rate has standard deviation $\sigma = 12\,\text{Hz}$ across trials.
    a) Compute the SEM for $n = 36$ trials.
    b) How many trials are needed for an SEM of $1\,\text{Hz}$?
15. **Neuro (diffusion):** Use $x = \sqrt{2Dt}$ with $D = 0.5\,\mu\text{m}^2/\text{ms}$.
    a) How far does a molecule diffuse in $t = 100\,\text{ms}$?
    b) How long does it take to diffuse $20\,\mu\text{m}$?
16. 🧮 **Neuro (RMS):** An EEG snippet has the samples $3, -4, 5, -2\;\mu\text{V}$. Compute the RMS amplitude $\sqrt{\tfrac1n\sum x_i^2}$.
