# A1 Exponent Rules – Worksheet

> Theory: [[A1 Exponent Rules]] · Solutions: [[A1 Exponent Rules - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Basics]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮.

## Level 1 – Warm-up
1. Simplify $a^3 \cdot a^5$.
2. Simplify $\dfrac{x^7}{x^2}$.
3. Simplify $(y^3)^4$.
4. Compute $2^{-3}$ and $5^0$.
5. Compute $10^{3} \cdot 10^{-5}$.

## Level 2 – Standard
6. Simplify $(2a^2 b)^3$.
7. Compute $8^{2/3}$.
8. Compute $16^{-3/4}$.
9. Simplify $\dfrac{x^{-2} y^3}{x^3 y^{-1}}$ and write the result without negative exponents.
10. Compute $(3 \times 10^{4}) \cdot (2 \times 10^{-7})$ in scientific notation.
11. Compute $\dfrac{4.8 \times 10^{-6}}{1.2 \times 10^{-9}}$.
12. Simplify $\left(a^{1/2}\, b^{-1/3}\right)^6$.

## Level 3 – Applied & tricky
13. Solve for $x$: $2^{x+1} = 32$.
14. Solve for $x$: $3^{2x} \cdot 9 = 81^{x}$.
15. Simplify $\dfrac{(x^{n+1})^2}{x^{2n-1}}$.
16. **Neuro (Ohm's law):** A current of $I = 200\,\text{pA}$ flows into a neuron with input resistance $R = 250\,\text{M}\Omega$. Compute the voltage change $V = IR$ in mV.
17. **Neuro (power law):** The power spectrum of resting EEG follows approximately $P(f) = c \cdot f^{-2}$. By which factor does the power change when the frequency is doubled? When it is multiplied by 10?
18. 🧮 **Neuro:** The human cortex contains about $1.5 \times 10^{14}$ synapses and the brain about $8.6 \times 10^{10}$ neurons. Estimate the average number of synapses per neuron (first as an order of magnitude without calculator, then exactly).
