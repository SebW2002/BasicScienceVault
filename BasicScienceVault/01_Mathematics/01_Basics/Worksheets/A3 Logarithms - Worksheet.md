# A3 Logarithms – Worksheet

> Theory: [[A3 Logarithms]] · Solutions: [[A3 Logarithms - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Basics]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮.

## Level 1 – Evaluate
1. $\log_2 32$
2. $\log_{10} 0.001$
3. $\log_3 \tfrac19$
4. $\ln e^5$ and $e^{\ln 7}$
5. $\log_4 8$

## Level 2 – Rules
6. Expand: $\log\!\left(\dfrac{x^2 y}{z}\right)$
7. Write as a single logarithm: $2\ln a - \ln b + \tfrac12 \ln c$
8. Compute $\log_2 12 - \log_2 3$.
9. Compute $\log_6 4 + \log_6 9$.
10. Express $\log_2 10$ using $\ln$ (change of base).

## Level 3 – Equations
11. Solve $\log_2 x = 7$.
12. Solve $5^x = 125$ and $2^x = 10$ (exact form).
13. Solve $\log_3(x+1) + \log_3(x-1) = 1$. Check the domain!

## Level 4 – Applications
14. **Decibels:** a) A signal has 1000 times the power of the reference. How many dB? b) An amplitude ratio corresponds to 40 dB. What is the amplitude ratio?
15. **Information:** How many bits are needed to identify one of 8 equally likely stimuli? Of 1024?
16. **Nernst:** Use $E = 61.5\,\text{mV} \cdot \frac{1}{z} \log_{10}\frac{c_\text{out}}{c_\text{in}}$.
    a) Na⁺ ($z = 1$): $c_\text{out} = 145\,\text{mM}$, $c_\text{in} = 14.5\,\text{mM}$.
    b) Ca²⁺ ($z = 2$): $c_\text{out} = 2\,\text{mM}$, $c_\text{in} = 0.0002\,\text{mM}$.
17. 🧮 **Nernst:** K⁺ ($z=1$) with $c_\text{out} = 4\,\text{mM}$, $c_\text{in} = 140\,\text{mM}$.
