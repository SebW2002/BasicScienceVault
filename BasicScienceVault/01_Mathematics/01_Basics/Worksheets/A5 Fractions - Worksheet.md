# A5 Fractions – Worksheet

> Theory: [[A5 Fractions]] · Solutions: [[A5 Fractions - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Basics]]
> No calculator needed.

## Level 1 – Numbers
1. $\dfrac23 + \dfrac34$
2. $\dfrac56 - \dfrac14$
3. $\dfrac35 \cdot \dfrac{10}{9}$
4. $\dfrac47 : \dfrac{2}{21}$
5. $\dfrac{\frac12 + \frac13}{\frac56}$

## Level 2 – Variables
6. $\dfrac1x + \dfrac1{2x}$
7. $\dfrac ab + \dfrac ba$
8. $\dfrac{1}{x-1} - \dfrac{1}{x+1}$
9. $\dfrac{2}{x-2} - \dfrac1x$
10. $\dfrac{x}{x+1} \cdot \dfrac{x^2-1}{x^2}$
11. $\dfrac{1 + \frac1x}{1 - \frac1x}$

## Level 3 – Rearranging
12. Solve $f = \dfrac{c}{c + K}$ for $K$.
13. Solve $\dfrac1R = \dfrac1{R_1} + \dfrac1{R_2}$ for $R$.

## Level 4 – Neuro applications
14. **Parallel resistances:** a) Two membrane patches with $100\,\text{M}\Omega$ each in parallel. Total $R$? b) Three paths with $60$, $30$ and $20\,\text{M}\Omega$ in parallel?
15. **Hill function** $f(c) = \dfrac{c^2}{c^2 + K^2}$: compute $f$ for $c = K$, $c = 3K$ and $c = K/2$.
16. **Divisive normalisation:** $R_1 = \dfrac{r_1}{\sigma + r_1 + r_2}$ with $r_1 = 6$, $r_2 = 2$, $\sigma = 2$. What happens to $R_1$ if $r_2$ rises to 12?
