# A7 Sums, Products and Factorials – Worksheet

> Theory: [[A7 Sums Products and Factorials]] · Solutions: [[A7 Sums Products and Factorials - Solutions]] · Log mistakes in [[Mistakes - Basics]]
> No calculator unless marked 🧮.

## Level 1 – Evaluate
1. $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{4} i^2$
2. $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{3} 2^k$
3. $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{5} 3$
4. $\displaystyle\prod_{i=1}^{4} i$ and $\displaystyle\prod_{k=1}^{3} 2^k$
5. $\dfrac{6!}{4!}$, $\;\dfrac{10!}{8!\,2!}$, $\;0!$

## Level 2 – Notation & rules
6. Compute $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{100} i$.
7. Write in $\Sigma$ notation and evaluate: $1 + 3 + 5 + \dots + 19$.
8. Simplify $\dfrac{(n+1)!}{n!}$.
9. Shift the index so the sum starts at $j = 1$, then evaluate: $\displaystyle\sum_{i=2}^{6} (i-1)^2$.
10. Given $\sum_{i=1}^{5} x_i = 20$ and $\sum_{i=1}^{5} y_i = 5$, compute $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{5} (3x_i - 2y_i + 1)$.
11. Compute $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{5} \left(\tfrac12\right)^k$ and $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \left(\tfrac12\right)^k$.
12. Simplify $\ln\left(\displaystyle\prod_{i=1}^{3} e^{i}\right)$.

## Level 3 – Neuro
13. **Statistics:** firing rates (Hz) across 8 trials: $2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9$. Compute the mean and the population variance $\frac1n\sum(x_i - \bar x)^2$.
14. **Perceptron:** weights $w = (0.5, -1, 2)$, inputs $x = (4, 1, 3)$. Compute $u = \sum w_i x_i$. Does the neuron fire with threshold $\theta = 5$?
15. **Poisson:** a neuron fires on average $\lambda = 2$ spikes per window. Give exact expressions for $P(0)$, $P(2)$ and $P(\text{at most } 1)$.
16. 🧮 **Poisson:** $\lambda = 3$, compute $P(k = 4)$.
