## Level 1 – Expand
1. $(x + 3)^2$
2. $(2a - 5)^2$
3. $(3x + 4y)(3x - 4y)$
4. $\left(\tfrac12 x + 2\right)^2$

## Level 2 – Factor & mental arithmetic
5. Factor $x^2 - 10x + 25$.
6. Factor $49 - 4y^2$.
7. Factor $9a^2 + 12ab + 4b^2$.
8. Compute without calculator: $101^2$, $\;98 \cdot 102$, $\;49^2$.
9. Complete the square: $x^2 + 6x + 2$ and $x^2 - 5x$.
10. Show that $(a + b)^2 - (a - b)^2 = 4ab$.

## Level 3 – Binomial theorem
11. Compute $\binom52$, $\binom63$, $\binom{10}{0}$, $\binom{10}{9}$.
12. Expand $(a + b)^4$.
13. Expand $(x - 2)^3$.
14. Find the coefficient of $x^3$ in $(1 + 2x)^5$.
15. Approximate $1.01^5$ using the first three terms of the binomial theorem.

## Level 4 – Neuro
16. **Quantal release:** a synapse has $n = 4$ release sites, each with $p = \tfrac12$.
    a) Probability that exactly 2 vesicles are released?
    b) Probability that at least one vesicle is released?
    c) Mean number of released vesicles?
17. 🧮 Same model with $n = 10$, $p = 0.3$: compute $P(k = 3)$.
