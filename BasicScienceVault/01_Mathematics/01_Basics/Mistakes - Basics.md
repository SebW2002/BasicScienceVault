## Classic traps (pre-filled)
| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Quick test | Topic |
|---|---|---|---|
| $(a+b)^2 = a^2 + b^2$ | $a^2 + 2ab + b^2$ | $a=b=1$: $4 \neq 2$ | [[A6 Binomial Formulas and Theorem]] |
| $\sqrt{a+b} = \sqrt a + \sqrt b$ | no simplification | $\sqrt{9+16}=5 \neq 7$ | [[A2 Root Rules]] |
| $\ln(a+b) = \ln a + \ln b$ | $\ln(ab) = \ln a + \ln b$ | $a=b=1$: $\ln 2 \neq 0$ | [[A3 Logarithms]] |
| $\ln a \cdot \ln b = \ln(ab)$ | $\ln(ab) = \ln a + \ln b$ | $a = b = e$: $1 \neq 2$ | [[A3 Logarithms]] |
| $a^m \cdot a^n = a^{mn}$ | $a^{m+n}$ | $2^2 \cdot 2^3 = 32 \neq 64$ | [[A1 Exponent Rules]] |
| $-2^2 = 4$ | $-2^2 = -4$, $(-2)^2 = 4$ | brackets! | [[A1 Exponent Rules]] |
| $(2x)^3 = 2x^3$ | $8x^3$ | $x=1$: $8 \neq 2$ | [[A1 Exponent Rules]] |
| $\dfrac{x+2}{2} = x + 1$ | $\dfrac x2 + 1$ | $x=2$: $2 \neq 3$ | [[A4 Factoring and Simplifying]] |
| $\dfrac{1}{a+b} = \dfrac1a + \dfrac1b$ | no simplification | $a=b=1$: $\tfrac12 \neq 2$ | [[A5 Fractions]] |
| $\dfrac ab + \dfrac cd = \dfrac{a+c}{b+d}$ | $\dfrac{ad+bc}{bd}$ | $\tfrac12+\tfrac12 = 1 \neq \tfrac24$ | [[A5 Fractions]] |
| $\sqrt{x^2} = x$ | $\lvert x \rvert$ | $x = -3$ | [[A2 Root Rules]] |
| $x^2 = 2x \Rightarrow x = 2$ | $x \in \{0, 2\}$ | don't divide by $x$ | [[A8 Solving Equations]] |
| Forgot to check after squaring | always substitute back | – | [[A8 Solving Equations]] |
| $-2x > 6 \Rightarrow x > -3$ | $x < -3$ | test $x = 0$ | [[A8 Solving Equations]] |
| $\sum a_i b_i = \sum a_i \sum b_i$ | not equal | $a=b=(1,1)$: $2 \neq 4$ | [[A7 Sums Products and Factorials]] |
| $\sum_{i=1}^n c = c$ | $n \cdot c$ | – | [[A7 Sums Products and Factorials]] |
| $0! = 0$ | $0! = 1$ | – | [[A7 Sums Products and Factorials]] |
| Mixed up mV and V, pA and nA | convert to powers of 10 first | – | [[A1 Exponent Rules]] |
