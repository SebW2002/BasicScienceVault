# A1 Exponent Rules – Solutions

> Worksheet: [[A1 Exponent Rules - Worksheet]] · Theory: [[A1 Exponent Rules]] · Wrong? → [[Mistakes - Basics]]

## Level 1
1. $a^{3+5} = a^8$
2. $x^{7-2} = x^5$
3. $y^{3 \cdot 4} = y^{12}$
4. $2^{-3} = \dfrac{1}{2^3} = \dfrac18$; $\;5^0 = 1$
5. $10^{3+(-5)} = 10^{-2} = 0.01$

## Level 2
6. $2^3 (a^2)^3 b^3 = 8a^6 b^3$
7. $8^{2/3} = (\sqrt[3]{8})^2 = 2^2 = 4$
8. $16^{-3/4} = \dfrac{1}{(\sqrt[4]{16})^3} = \dfrac{1}{2^3} = \dfrac18$
9. $x^{-2-3} y^{3-(-1)} = x^{-5} y^{4} = \dfrac{y^4}{x^5}$
10. $(3 \cdot 2) \times 10^{4-7} = 6 \times 10^{-3}$
11. $\dfrac{4.8}{1.2} \times 10^{-6-(-9)} = 4 \times 10^{3}$
12. $a^{6/2} b^{-6/3} = a^3 b^{-2} = \dfrac{a^3}{b^2}$

## Level 3
13. $32 = 2^5 \Rightarrow x + 1 = 5 \Rightarrow x = 4$
14. Write everything as powers of 3: $3^{2x} \cdot 3^2 = (3^4)^x \Rightarrow 3^{2x+2} = 3^{4x} \Rightarrow 2x + 2 = 4x \Rightarrow x = 1$
15. $\dfrac{x^{2n+2}}{x^{2n-1}} = x^{(2n+2)-(2n-1)} = x^{3}$
16. $V = 200 \times 10^{-12}\,\text{A} \cdot 250 \times 10^{6}\,\Omega = 50\,000 \times 10^{-6}\,\text{V} = 5 \times 10^{-2}\,\text{V} = 50\,\text{mV}$
17. $P(2f) = c(2f)^{-2} = 2^{-2} \cdot c f^{-2} = \tfrac14 P(f)$ → power drops to **one quarter**. With $10f$: factor $10^{-2} = \tfrac{1}{100}$.
18. Order of magnitude: $\dfrac{10^{14}}{10^{11}} = 10^{3}$ (thousands). Exactly: $\dfrac{1.5}{8.6} \times 10^{4} \approx 0.1744 \times 10^4 \approx 1744$ synapses per neuron (on average – real numbers vary enormously between cell types).
