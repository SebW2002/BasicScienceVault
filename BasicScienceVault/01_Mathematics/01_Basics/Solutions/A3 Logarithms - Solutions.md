# A3 Logarithms – Solutions

> Worksheet: [[A3 Logarithms - Worksheet]] · Theory: [[A3 Logarithms]] · Wrong? → [[Mistakes - Basics]]

## Level 1
1. $2^5 = 32 \Rightarrow 5$
2. $10^{-3} = 0.001 \Rightarrow -3$
3. $3^{-2} = \tfrac19 \Rightarrow -2$
4. $5$ and $7$ (log and exp cancel)
5. $4^x = 8 \Leftrightarrow 2^{2x} = 2^3 \Rightarrow x = \tfrac32$

## Level 2
6. $2\log x + \log y - \log z$
7. $\ln a^2 - \ln b + \ln\sqrt c = \ln\dfrac{a^2\sqrt c}{b}$
8. $\log_2\tfrac{12}{3} = \log_2 4 = 2$
9. $\log_6(4\cdot9) = \log_6 36 = 2$
10. $\log_2 10 = \dfrac{\ln 10}{\ln 2}$ ($\approx 3.32$)

## Level 3
11. $x = 2^7 = 128$
12. $125 = 5^3 \Rightarrow x = 3$. $\;2^x = 10 \Rightarrow x = \log_2 10 = \dfrac{\ln10}{\ln2}$
13. Domain: $x + 1 > 0$ and $x - 1 > 0$ → $x > 1$.
    $\log_3\big((x+1)(x-1)\big) = 1 \Rightarrow x^2 - 1 = 3 \Rightarrow x = \pm2$. Only $x = 2$ lies in the domain → **$x = 2$**.

## Level 4
14. a) $10\log_{10}1000 = 10 \cdot 3 = 30\,\text{dB}$. b) $20\log_{10}(A/A_0) = 40 \Rightarrow \log_{10}(A/A_0) = 2 \Rightarrow A/A_0 = 100$.
15. $\log_2 8 = 3$ bits; $\log_2 1024 = 10$ bits.
16. a) $61.5 \cdot \log_{10}10 = +61.5\,\text{mV}$
    b) Ratio $= 2/0.0002 = 10^4$ → $\dfrac{61.5}{2}\cdot 4 = 123\,\text{mV}$
17. $61.5 \cdot \log_{10}(4/140) = 61.5 \cdot \log_{10}(0.02857) \approx 61.5 \cdot (-1.544) \approx -95\,\text{mV}$
