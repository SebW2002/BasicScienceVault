# A7 Sums, Products and Factorials – Solutions

> Worksheet: [[A7 Sums Products and Factorials - Worksheet]] · Theory: [[A7 Sums Products and Factorials]] · Wrong? → [[Mistakes - Basics]]

## Level 1
1. $1 + 4 + 9 + 16 = 30$
2. $1 + 2 + 4 + 8 = 15$
3. $5 \cdot 3 = 15$
4. $1\cdot2\cdot3\cdot4 = 24$; $\;2^1 \cdot 2^2 \cdot 2^3 = 2^{6} = 64$
5. $6 \cdot 5 = 30$; $\;\dfrac{10\cdot9}{2} = 45$; $\;0! = 1$

## Level 2
6. $\dfrac{100 \cdot 101}{2} = 5050$
7. $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{10} (2k - 1) = 2\cdot\frac{10\cdot11}{2} - 10 = 110 - 10 = 100$ (sum of the first $n$ odd numbers is $n^2$)
8. $\dfrac{(n+1)\cdot n!}{n!} = n + 1$
9. $j = i - 1$: $\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{5} j^2 = \frac{5\cdot6\cdot11}{6} = 55$
10. $3\cdot20 - 2\cdot5 + 5\cdot1 = 60 - 10 + 5 = 55$ (don't forget: the constant 1 is summed 5 times!)
11. $\tfrac12 + \tfrac14 + \tfrac18 + \tfrac1{16} + \tfrac1{32} = \tfrac{31}{32}$; infinite: $\dfrac{1}{1 - 1/2} = 2$
12. $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{3} \ln e^i = 1 + 2 + 3 = 6$

## Level 3
13. Mean: $\frac{40}{8} = 5$. Deviations squared: $9, 1, 1, 1, 0, 0, 4, 16$ → sum 32 → variance $\frac{32}{8} = 4$ (SD = 2).
14. $u = 2 - 1 + 6 = 7 > 5$ → the neuron fires.
15. $P(0) = e^{-2}$; $\;P(2) = \dfrac{2^2 e^{-2}}{2!} = 2e^{-2}$; $\;P(\le1) = e^{-2} + 2e^{-2} = 3e^{-2}$
16. $\dfrac{3^4 e^{-3}}{4!} = \dfrac{81}{24} e^{-3} = 3.375 \cdot 0.0498 \approx 0.168$
