## Level 2
5. $(x - 5)^2$
6. $(7 - 2y)(7 + 2y)$

8. $(100 + 1)^2 = 10000 + 200 + 1 = 10201$; $\;(100-2)(100+2) = 10000 - 4 = 9996$; $\;(50-1)^2 = 2401$

10. $(a^2 + 2ab + b^2) - (a^2 - 2ab + b^2) = 4ab$ ✓

## Level 3
11. $\binom52 = \frac{5\cdot4}{2} = 10$; $\binom63 = \frac{6\cdot5\cdot4}{6} = 20$; $\binom{10}0 = 1$; $\binom{10}9 = 10$
12. $a^4 + 4a^3b + 6a^2b^2 + 4ab^3 + b^4$
13. $x^3 + 3x^2(-2) + 3x(-2)^2 + (-2)^3 = x^3 - 6x^2 + 12x - 8$
14. Term with $k = 3$: $\binom53 (2x)^3 = 10 \cdot 8x^3$ → coefficient **80**
15. $(1 + 0.01)^5 \approx 1 + 5(0.01) + 10(0.01)^2 = 1 + 0.05 + 0.001 = 1.051$ (exact: 1.05101…)
