# A4 Factoring and Simplifying – Solutions

> Worksheet: [[A4 Factoring and Simplifying - Worksheet]] · Theory: [[A4 Factoring and Simplifying]] · Wrong? → [[Mistakes - Basics]]

## Level 1
1. $3(2x + 3)$
2. $2ab(2a - 3b)$
3. $x(x^2 - 1) = x(x-1)(x+1)$
4. $a(x+y) + b(x+y) = (a+b)(x+y)$

## Level 2
5. Product 6, sum 5 → $(x+2)(x+3)$
6. Product −12, sum −1 → $(x-4)(x+3)$
7. $2(x^2 - 4) = 2(x-2)(x+2)$
8. Zeros: $x = \frac{-7 \pm \sqrt{49 - 24}}{4} = \frac{-7\pm5}{4}$ → $-\tfrac12$ and $-3$ → $2(x + \tfrac12)(x+3) = (2x+1)(x+3)$
9. $(x^2 - 4)(x^2 + 4) = (x-2)(x+2)(x^2+4)$ ($x^2 + 4$ has no real zeros)

## Level 3
10. $\dfrac{(x-3)(x+3)}{x+3} = x - 3$, $\;x \neq -3$
11. $\dfrac{x(x+2)}{(x-2)(x+2)} = \dfrac{x}{x-2}$, $\;x \neq \pm2$
12. $\dfrac{a(a-b)}{b(a-b)} = \dfrac ab$, $\;b \neq 0,\ a \neq b$
13. Numerator: $\dfrac{y - x}{xy} = -\dfrac{x-y}{xy}$. Divide by $(x-y)$: $-\dfrac{1}{xy}$, $\;x, y \neq 0,\ x \neq y$

## Level 4
14. A summand was "cancelled". Correct: $\dfrac{x+2}{2} = \dfrac x2 + 1$. (Test with $x = 2$: left $2$, "result" $3$ ✗.)
15. a) $V(g_L + g_s) - g_L E_L - g_s E_s = 0 \Rightarrow V = \dfrac{g_L E_L + g_s E_s}{g_L + g_s}$
    b) $V = \dfrac{10\cdot(-70) + 10\cdot 0}{20} = -35\,\text{mV}$ (exactly halfway, equal weights)
    c) Divide numerator and denominator by $g_s$: $V = \dfrac{(g_L/g_s)E_L + E_s}{(g_L/g_s) + 1} \to E_s$. A very strong synapse clamps the membrane to its reversal potential.
